## Current in a semiconductor
n-type silicon: ND=5×10¹⁷ cm⁻³, μn=700 cm²/(V·s), T=300 K, q=1.602×10⁻¹⁹ C. Cross-section 10 μm×10 μm, length 0.1 cm, applied voltage 10 V. The solution finds σ≈56.07 (Ω·cm)⁻¹, R≈1783 Ω, I≈5.6 mA.

## Diode breakdown
Emax=300 kV/cm, VR=1200 V, NA≫ND and VR≫V₀. From the relation Emax²=2qNDVR/εs, ND≈2.42×10¹⁴ cm⁻³. εs=11.7ε₀ is used.

## Clippers and Zener regulator
In the clippers with silicon diodes, the first output is limited at +4.7 V, and the second output is limited at +3.7 and −2.7 V. In the Zener question, the load is 2–4 Ω, the target output is 6 V, the input is 15–20 V, and the minimum Zener current is 50 mA. The solution gives approximately 3 Ω for the source series resistance and approximately 3.2 A for the maximum Zener current.

## FET operating point
In the circuit with a 10 V supply, the gate divider is 5 kΩ and 3 kΩ, RD=RS=4 kΩ, kn=0.5 mA/V², VTN=−2 V. VG=3.75 V. The solution finds source VGS≈−0.05 V, ID≈0.95 mA, VDS≈2.4 V, and checks the saturation condition.

In the JFET design, IDSS=6 mA, VP=−3 V, target ID=2 mA and VDS=5 V; supply 8 V. The solution gives VGS≈−1.3 V, RS≈650 Ω, RD≈850 Ω, and recommends R₁≫10 kΩ for the gate resistance.
