The **sample entrepreneur assignment** prepared for the SOS204 Business course: a student describes an entrepreneur they feel close to and tells that person's entrepreneurial story. The text of the assignment is below; a PDF with the names redacted can be downloaded at the end of the post.

*Note: The name of the person in the assignment has been changed ("Murat Demirtaş" is a fictitious name here); the names in the downloadable PDF are also redacted. The text reflects the views and accounts of the student who wrote the assignment; its accuracy has not been separately verified.*

## Murat Demirtaş

Among well-known entrepreneurs, Murat Demirtaş is the one who most caught the attention of the student who prepared the assignment. The most fundamental reason is that he resonates with the student: Murat Demirtaş is also a graduate of a faculty of engineering. Despite an undergraduate degree that could be considered unsuccessful, like many others, he achieved what none of his friends managed to: he rose to a position at an international technology company with responsibility for Turkey. When asked how he came to this position, he said, "You don't apply, they make you an offer." According to the student, his entrepreneurial life begins right here.

When he starts university, before Facebook has fully reached Turkey, he sets up a student forum. According to the student, this is a move so accessible that anyone could have pulled it off. Later, following a warning from the university, the forum, which was "completely" free, is shut down. However, this first venture is taken into consideration years later by the company he would go on to work for.

Later, before graduating, he prepares another project to obtain a public grant. With the backing of ministry support, he develops a feature for websites that allows underlining, as one does in books, but it fails. This is because people do not read on the internet either; they look at the pictures and move on. He explained this by saying, "You can't calculate everything."

He doesn't prepare for the KPSS (the public-sector job exam) either, changes jobs frequently, and tries to improve himself. Shortly afterwards, that technology company makes him an offer, taking his past projects into account, and from then on he works as an investor, active in various ventures.
