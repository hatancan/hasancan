## Laboratory information form (2010-2011 Spring)

### A) How the laboratory works

1. Each experiment consists of a **pre-lab assignment**, an **experimental work** session carried out during the lab hours, and a **report** submitted within one week.
1. Pre-lab assignments are obtained from the web page one week before the lab day and submitted within the first 10 minutes of the experiment. A student who comes without having completed the pre-lab assignment cannot perform the experiment and receives zero for that experiment; if it is later discovered that a student did not submit the pre-lab assignment, their grade for that experiment is also zero.
1. Submitted pre-lab assignments are not returned. In software labs, it is the student's responsibility to bring a copy of the pre-lab assignment on a medium such as a floppy disk, CD, or flash drive.
1. Each student performs the experiments individually; group work is not permitted except in cases of necessity.
1. At the start of the experiment, the student checks that the equipment is working and reports any malfunction to the teaching assistant.
1. A surprise short quiz may be given during the experiment session; if given, it makes up 50% of the Performance and Oral grade.
1. The student shows the completed work to the assistant on duty; questions may be asked or changes requested if necessary. At the end, the student returns the materials and leaves the desk tidy.
1. Reports must be submitted within one week at the latest, in the specified format. Late penalties: 1 day 20%, 2 days 30%, 3 days 50%; for 4 days or more, the lab grade is zero.
1. 80% attendance in the laboratories is mandatory; excessive absence without an official excuse means failing the course.
1. A student with an official excuse approved by the department is granted a make-up opportunity.
1. A student whose laboratory average at the end of the semester is below 25/100 is considered to have failed the course.

### B) General rules

- A student who is more than 10 minutes late to the experiment is not admitted to that class hour; they may arrive at the beginning of the second hour and perform the experiment without being given extra time. If the delay was caused by the shuttle service, the student may join later with a letter obtained from the relevant unit.
- Walking around the laboratory and leaving without permission during the experiment are prohibited.
- Eating and drinking in the laboratory are prohibited.
- Each student is responsible for the experiment kit and materials they use.
- The experiment grade of a student who cheats or lets others cheat is zero.

### C) Grading

| Pre-lab | Experimental work | Performance and oral | Report |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20% | 20% | 30% | 30% |

If any component is not applicable, the grade is converted to a 100-point scale. For example, an experiment without a pre-lab assignment is graded out of 70; a student who scores 35/70 receives 50/100.
