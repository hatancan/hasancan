## Divergence theorem
For A = r³âr−10zâz, ∇·A = 4r²−10. Over the volume 1≤r≤2, 0≤φ≤2π, 0≤z≤5, the total volume integral is zero. The boundary fluxes are −10π at r=1, 160π at r=2, zero at z=0, and −150π at z=5; the total is again zero. Although the net source is zero, the local source density is not zero at every point.

## Stokes' theorem
For the field A = yâx+zây, the four edge integrals around the square −1≤x,y≤1 in the z=0 plane are 0, −2, 0, −2, respectively. The total is −4. ∇×A = −âx−âz; the surface integral with the +z normal is also −4. Since the curl is not zero, the field cannot be written as the gradient of a scalar potential.

## Total field of a surface and line charge
In the solution, the contribution of the infinite surface is 6âz, and the contribution of the line charge is 8ây−6âz V/m. The distance to the line is 5, and the unit vector from the line to the observation point is (−4ây+3âz)/5. The result of superposition is E = 8ây V/m.
