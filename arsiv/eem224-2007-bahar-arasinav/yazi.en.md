## Charged cylinder and conducting shell
ρ=3rε₀ in the region 0≤r≤1. Air for 1<r≤2 and r>3, perfect conductor for 2<r<3. By Gauss's law, E=r²âr (r<1), E=âr/r (1<r<2), E=0 (2<r<3), E=âr/r (r>3).

V(r)−V(3):

- r<1: (1−r³)/3+ln2.
- 1<r<2: ln(2/r).
- 2<r<3: 0.
- r>3: ln(3/r).

## Dielectric boundary
z<0: ε₁=4ε₀; z>0: ε₂=2ε₀. E₁=1.5âx−2ây+âz. D₁=ε₀(6âx−8ây+4âz), P₁=ε₀(4.5âx−6ây+3âz). E₂=1.5âx−2ây+2âz, D₂=ε₀(3âx−4ây+4âz), P₂=ε₀(1.5âx−2ây+2âz). Interface polarization charge ε₀.

## Directional derivative
V=2yz−x². At (2,1,3), ∇V=−4âx+6ây+2âz. The unit vector pointing toward (−1,2,2) is (−3âx+ây−âz)/√11. The rate of change in this direction is 16/√11.

## Polarized cube
For a cube with its center at the origin and edge length 1 m, P=xâx+yây+zâz. ρpv=−3 C/m³; on each face, relative to the outward normal, ρps=1/2 C/m². Total volume charge is −3 C and surface charge is 3 C; net is zero.
