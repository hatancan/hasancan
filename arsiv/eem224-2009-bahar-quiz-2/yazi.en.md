## Variable Dielectric and Free Space
In the region 0<y<ln4, ε(y)=8ε₀e⁻ʸ; in the region ln4<y<ln4+1/8, free space. V₀=4 V and the plate area is 1 m². From the continuity of D and the voltage integral, D=8ε₀ây. In the dielectric E=eʸây, in free space E=8ây. P=ε₀(8−eʸ)ây; ρpv=ε₀eʸ.

The volume polarization charge is 3ε₀; −7ε₀ on the lower surface and 4ε₀ on the upper surface. The net polarization charge is zero. C=2ε₀, energy 16ε₀ J. The pressure at the dielectric–air interface is 16ε₀ N/m², in the +y direction; the pressure at y=0 is 4ε₀ N/m², in the −y direction.

## Conducting Medium Boundary
x<0: ε₁=4ε₀, σ₁=2; x>0: ε₂=3ε₀, σ₂=1. J₁=4âx+3ây. From the continuity of normal J and tangential E:

- E₁=2âx+1.5ây; D₁=ε₀(8âx+6ây); P₁=ε₀(6âx+4.5ây).
- J₂=4âx+1.5ây; E₂ has the same numerical components; D₂=ε₀(12âx+4.5ây); P₂=ε₀(8âx+3ây).
- θ₁≈37°, cotθ₂=8/3.
- The free surface charge is 4ε₀, and the total polarization surface charge is −2ε₀.

## Coaxial Magnetic Structure
A uniform current I in the inner conductor of radius a=1 m; μ=2μ₀r in the region 1<r<2; an opposite current I in the outer conductor at r=2. Hφ=Ir/(2π) (r<1), Hφ=I/(2πr) (1<r<2), zero outside. B is μ₀Ir/(2π) in the inner conductor, μ₀I/π in the magnetic material, and zero outside.

M=(2r−1)I/(2πr) âφ; Jm=I/(πr) âz. The equivalent surface current on the inner surface is I/(2π) âz, and on the outer surface −3I/(4π) âz. The total equivalent volume current is 2I, the inner surface current is I, and the outer surface current is −3I; the net is zero.
