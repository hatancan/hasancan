Four **course content forms** (dated 28.09.2007, 27.09.2010, 25.09.2013, 28.09.2015) for the course **EEM335 Computer Organization**, taught by Prof. Dr. Emin Akata in the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Başkent University. The order of topics changed somewhat from year to year; a summary of the four forms is given below. The original PDFs can be downloaded as a ZIP at the end of the post.

The textbook in all of them is **W. Stallings, *Computer Organization & Architecture***.

## Grade breakdown

| Year | Attendance | Quiz | Homework | 1st midterm | Final exam |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2007-2008 fall | 5% | 15% | 10% | 30% | 40% |
| 2010-2011 | 5% | 20% | 5% | 30% | 40% |
| 2013-2014 fall | 5% | 25% (including homework and presentation) | – | 30% | 40% |
| 2015-2016 fall | 5% | 25% (including homework and presentation) | – | 30% | 40% |

In 2007, class hours were Monday 14:00–15:50 and Wednesday 14:00–15:50 (office hours Tuesday 15:00–16:50); in 2010, Tuesday 09:00–10:50 and Wednesday 14:00–15:50; in 2015, Monday 15:00–16:50 and Wednesday 13:00–14:50.

## Weekly topics (2013-2014 fall form)

| Week | Topic |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | Organization and architecture, historical development of the computer, computer structure, basic definitions |
| 3-4 | Computer operation, interconnections and bus structure, PCI |
| 5-6 | Memory, cache memory, cache design and organization |
| 7 | Main memory, DRAM, SRAM, ROM, error correction, DRAM organization |
| 8 | Midterm exam |
| 9-10 | Magnetic disk and its organization, optical disk structures |
| 11-12 | I/O architecture, programmed I/O, interrupt-driven I/O, DMA, I/O channels and processors |
| 13 | Operating system, job scheduling, memory management, virtual memory |
| 14 | Arithmetic logic unit, types of arithmetic operations and number representations |

## Differences between the years

- **2007-2008:** The cache section includes "Pentium 4 and Power PC cache organization"; the midterm is in week 9; the final weeks also include "machine language instruction structure" (weeks 14-15).
- **2010-2011:** The topics are compressed into 7 weeks (from week 1 to weeks 6-7); Pentium 4 and Power PC cache organization is mentioned again.
- **2013-2014 and 2015-2016:** The 14-week table above. In the 2015 form the PCI topic is not mentioned (it ends with "interconnections and bus structure").
