## Purpose and scope of the course

The EEM224 Electromagnetics I course covered time-invariant (static) electric and magnetic fields. EEM323 is its continuation and uses the same textbooks: static fields are briefly summarized, and then the main question is addressed: **what happens when there is a change in time?** When the charge distribution changes or the current increases and decreases over time, the assumption that electric and magnetic fields are "static" is no longer valid. The syllabus states that the work of Faraday, Ampère, Kirchhoff and especially Maxwell on this subject will be examined through examples.

- Semester: 23 September 2013 – 3 January 2014; final exam week begins 5 January.
- Class hours: Monday 13:00–14:50, Thursday 11:00–12:50. Office hours: Monday 15:00–16:50.

- Homework is assigned weekly and is due the following Monday; late submissions lose 20% of the homework grade for every 24 hours. No make-up exam is planned.

## Textbooks

1. David K. Cheng, *Fundamentals of Engineering Electromagnetics* (Addison-Wesley, 1993; Turkish translation by Palme Yayınları).
2. David K. Cheng, *Field and Wave Electromagnetics* (Turkish translation by Literatür Yayıncılık).
3. Marshall, DuBroff, Skitek, *Electromagnetic Concepts and Applications*, 4th edition, Prentice Hall, 1996.

## Objectives

According to the syllabus, a student completing the course will grasp the physical interpretation of Maxwell's equations, appreciate the importance of mathematical approximations in simplifying nature, be able to describe the similarities and differences between the electromagnetic and optical wave equations, describe light as an electromagnetic wave, explain the relationship between a ray and a wavefront, describe the conditions for interference (constructive/destructive) and the use of a polarizer/analyzer, and unify the knowledge of electric and magnetic fields at 0 Hz with optical waves on the order of terahertz.

## 14-week topic plan (draft)

| Week | Topics |
|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction; vector algebra and operators; summary of static electric and magnetic fields |
| 2 | Time-varying fields; Faraday's law |
| 3 | Stationary circuit in a time-varying magnetic field; transformers; moving conductor in a static magnetic field |
| 4 | Maxwell's equations (differential and integral form); potential functions |
| 5 | Solution of wave equations; time-harmonic fields, phasors; Helmholtz equations |
| 6 | Plane electromagnetic waves; Doppler effect; wave polarization |
| 7 | Plane waves in lossy media |
| 8 | **Midterm exam** |
| 9 | Electromagnetic power; Poynting vector; instantaneous and average power density |
| 10 | Plane waves at normal incidence on planar boundaries; normal incidence on a good conductor |
| 11 | Oblique incidence; perpendicular and parallel polarization; Brewster angle |
| 12 | Transmission lines; general equations and parameters |
| 13 | Wave characteristics on infinite and finite transmission lines; open- and short-circuited lines |
| 14 | Reflection coefficient; standing wave ratio; Smith chart |
| 15 | **Final exam** |
