EEM214 Electronics I: Laboratory Information Booklet (2011–2012)

EEM214 – Elektronik I · 15.02.2012 · 16.10.2026

This post is the laboratory information booklet for the EEM214 Electronics I course of the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Başkent University (spring semester of the 2011–2012 academic year). The booklet explains how the laboratory operates, its rules, and the report format; its last page contains a blank cover sheet to be attached to preliminary work and reports. Only the first three weeks of the laboratory schedule are filled in, and it is noted that the final schedule will be announced later. The original Word file of the document can be downloaded at the end of the post.

How the laboratory operates

  1. Laboratory preliminary work is obtained from the relevant web page one week before the laboratory day, and students submit their preliminary work on the laboratory day. Students who arrive without having completed their preliminary work cannot do the laboratory work.
  2. Each student will perform the experiments on their own. Group work will not be permitted except in cases of necessity.
  3. The student is responsible for the operation of the materials or the experiment set to be used. The student is responsible for any condition during the experimental work that could affect the experiment result (such as a short circuit on the breadboard).
  4. At the end of the experimental work, students must show their completed work to the assistant on duty in the laboratory. The assistant on duty records each student's completed work; when necessary, they may ask the student questions about the work or ask the student to make changes to it.
  5. At the end of the experimental work, each student submits their preliminary work and the documents and materials related to the experimental work to the assistant on duty, and tidies up the laboratory equipment and the experiment bench.
  6. Experiment reports must be submitted at the latest by the next experiment, provided that they comply with the given format. The following penalties are applied to the report grade for late submissions: 1 day late −25%, 2 days late −50%, 3 or more days late: the report grade is zero.

Important: There is no make-up right for experiments that are not performed without an official excuse, and the corresponding experiment grade will be counted as 0. Students who cannot obtain at least 25% of the full mark as their laboratory grade, or who miss more than 20% of the laboratory hours without an official excuse, receive an F in the course.

General rules

  1. Students who arrive more than 10 minutes late to the laboratory hours are not allowed to enter the laboratory session they are late for. They may come to the next laboratory session to begin or continue the experiment.
  2. Walking around inside the laboratory and leaving without permission during the experiment is prohibited.
  3. Eating and drinking anything inside the laboratory is prohibited.
  4. Each student is responsible for the experiment set and the materials they work with.
  5. The laboratory grades of students who do not return the laboratory materials at the end of the experiment, or who leave without tidying up the experiment bench, are reduced by 10% for that week.

Preliminary work and report format

  • The attached cover sheet must be included in the preliminary work and reports to be prepared. Preliminary work and reports without a cover sheet will not be evaluated.
  • Except for work uploaded to the web page, all preliminary work and experiment reports are submitted written on white A4 paper. Reports or preliminary work written on notebook pages, scratch paper, or lined/squared paper will absolutely not be accepted.
  • What is requested in the preliminary work and report will be written clearly in itemized form.
  • Preliminary work and reports will be prepared in Turkish.
  • The total number of pages and the page number must be indicated on every page.
  • The preliminary work and reports to be submitted will be stapled with a wire stapler; they must not be submitted in a plastic sleeve folder. If unstapled work is lost, the responsibility lies with the student.

Warning: In whatever way it occurs, the corresponding experiment grades of students who cheat or allow others to copy are evaluated as zero, and the relevant disciplinary regulation is applied.

The booklet lists the names of the laboratory assistants as Res. Asst. Dilara Değerliyurt, Res. Asst. Deniz Kocaay, Res. Asst. Gamze Dağ, and Res. Asst. Gülsevin Kodaloğlu.

Cover sheet

The cover sheet is headed "Başkent University Electrical and Electronics Engineering – EEM 214 Electronics-I Laboratory Report/Preliminary Work" and contains the following fields: experiment name, experiment number, student name and surname, student number, experiment date, laboratory group number (Group 1 / Group 2), and laboratory classroom (A406 / A407).
