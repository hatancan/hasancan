INTERNSHIP Summer Internship: TCDD Annual Statistics 2009-2013 (Turkish State Railways Annual Statistics)

STAJ – Yaz Stajı · 06.08.2014 · 14.11.2026

This post introduces the book "İstatistik Yıllığı / Annual Statistics 2009-2013" (ISSN 1300-2503, August 2014, 139 pages, Turkish-English), published by the Statistics Branch of the Department of Research, Planning and Coordination of the Turkish State Railways (T.C. Devlet Demiryolları İşletmesi Genel Müdürlüğü, TCDD). The yearbook is the source of the line, electrification, signalling and telecommunications data used in the internship reports of students doing their summer internships at TCDD; the book, found in the internship folder, can be read together with the TCDD YHT internship reports. The complete yearbook can be downloaded as a PDF at the end of the post (the cover page has been simplified because of the contact details it contains).

This content belongs to a third-party source (T.C. Devlet Demiryolları İşletmesi Genel Müdürlüğü, Statistics Branch, 2014); it is shared for archival and nostalgic purposes with the source credited. It will be removed at the request of the rights holder.

Part I: General information

A brief history of TCDD, organization, mission, organization chart, TCDD map, affiliated companies, subsidiaries, factories, personnel and personnel training programs.

Part II: High-speed train (YHT)

High-speed train operations, the high-speed railway line (line information) and high-speed railway operations by country.

Part III: Current status

  • Track status: lengths of high-speed and conventional lines, distribution by region, electrification and electrified line lengths, telecommunications, signalled line lengths, line information, distribution of rails by age, rail and sleeper types, numbers and ages of tunnels, bridges and culverts, bridges and culverts by axle load, level crossings, track maintenance and repair units, track and work machinery.
  • Traction status: operating status of tractive vehicles, locomotive power and ages, technical specifications of electric locomotives and train sets and of diesel main-line locomotives, numbers and capacities of passenger vehicles, operating status, ages and capacities of freight wagons, rotation, locomotive and wagon maintenance and repair facilities.
  • Stations, depots and logistics directorates; suburban station and stop supervisorships.

Part IV: Operating activities

Gross ton-kilometres (by locomotive type, region, train type and line section), train-kilometres, locomotive-kilometres, seat-kilometres, wagon-kilometres, axle-kilometres, passenger transport and freight transport.

Part V: Ports

Port information, Lake Van ferry operations, port traffic, loading and unloading by year, distribution of cargo handled at ports by group, and income and expenditure of the ports.

Part VI: Financial status

Revenues, freight revenues by commodity type, TCDD general expenditure table, general expenditure table of railways and ports, energy consumption expenses, income-expenditure balance of operating activities and profit-and-loss statement.

Part VII: Investments

Physical progress of investments by project, stock quantities held in warehouses, and goods and services procurement tenders.

Part VIII: Productivity criteria and fuel consumption

Productivity criteria; other ratios, unit transport revenues, overall average costs and subsidy ratios, average freight haul distances by commodity type, revenue per ton-km of freight carried by commodity type, and fuel consumption.

Note: Because the yearbook's tables are bilingual and have many columns, they were not transcribed into the text; only the content headings are given. All tables are in the PDF.
