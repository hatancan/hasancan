This is a software overview presentation prepared by a friend for the EEM474 Power Electronics course at Başkent University. The instructor is stated as Hamit Erdem. Personal information has been removed; a cleaned PDF can be downloaded at the end of the post. No date is given.

## Simulation tools

| Software | Usage description given in the presentation | System and student edition stated in the presentation |
|---|---|---|
| MATLAB and Simulink | Graphical block diagrams, model-based design, control and digital signal processing | Windows; $89 |
| SuperSpice | Analog design, schematic capture and waveform viewing | Windows; £19 |
| PSIM / Power Sim | Power electronics and motor control simulation | Windows; free student edition |
| PSpice | Analog circuit and digital logic simulation | Windows; free student edition |
| PLECS | System-level electrical circuit simulation with a focus on power electronics | Windows, Linux, Mac; free student edition |
| NI Multisim and NI Ultiboard | Electronic schematic capture, circuit simulation and circuit design | Windows; free student edition |
| CoolSpice | Circuit simulation with schematic and text editors | Windows; free student edition |
| SAPWIN | Linear analog circuit simulation with symbolic analysis | Windows; free |
| LTspice | Integrated circuit and circuit simulation | Windows, Linux; free |
| NL5 | Electronic circuit and power supply design | Windows; free |
| Qucs | Circuit and noise behavior simulation | Windows, Linux, Mac; free |

*Note: The price, operating system and license information is the historical content of the presentation; it should not be used as current product information.*
