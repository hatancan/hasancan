## Plane wave in free space

The electric field is in the +y direction and the wave propagates in the +x direction. With frequency ω and real amplitude E₀:

Ẽ=E₀â_y e^(−jk₀x), H̃=(E₀/η₀)â_z e^(−jk₀x).

E=E₀â_y cos(ωt−k₀x), S=(E₀²/η₀)â_x cos²(ωt−k₀x).

## Lossy medium

μ=10μ₀, ε=72ε₀, σ=8 S/m; E(y,t)=10e^(−αy)cos(π10⁷t−βy)â_z V/m.

Since σ/(ωε)=400≫1, the good-conductor approximation is used. α≈β≈√(ωμσ/2)=4π√10. Phase velocity v_p=ω/β=2.5×10⁶/√10 m/s, λ=2π/β=1/(2√10) m, δ=1/α=1/(4π√10) m.

## Phase variation in two directions

Ẽ=4×10⁻⁴η₀â_z e^[−j(6x+8y)] V/m. The propagation unit vector is 0.6â_x+0.8â_y, β=10 rad/m; v=c, λ=0.2π m, ω=3×10⁹ rad/s.

H̃=4×10⁻⁴(0.8â_x−0.6â_y)e^[−j(6x+8y)] A/m. In the time-domain expression, the exponential phase is replaced by cos(3×10⁹t−6x−8y).

*Note: The source gives the direction angle as approximately 54°; arccos(0.6)=53.13° is computed.*

## Maxwell and continuity

The last question asks for ∇×E=−∂B/∂t, ∇×H=J+∂D/∂t, ∇·D=ρ, ∇·B=0 and ∇·J=−∂ρ/∂t to be written and their physical meanings explained.
