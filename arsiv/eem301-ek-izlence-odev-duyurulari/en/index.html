EEM301 Signals and Systems I: Supplementary Syllabus, Homework and Project Announcements, 2005–2017

EEM301 – Sinyaller ve Sistemler I · 28.09.2010 · 18.10.2026

This supplementary package collects the 2005 exercise list for EEM301, Alper Uraz's 2010–2011 fall syllabus dated 28 September 2010, the version of the homework with 30 sequential indices, and the project announcement whose archive modification date is 20 May 2017. These documents are different versions of the syllabi and homework assignments archived earlier. PDF copies of the four documents can be downloaded as a ZIP at the end of the post; student-related fields in the document metadata have been removed.

2010–2011 fall course information

  • Course: EEM301-01 Signals and Systems I.
  • Instructor: Prof. Dr. Alper Uraz.
  • Class hours: Monday 13.00–14.50, Thursday 11.00–12.50.
  • Office hours: Tuesday 11.00–11.50, Wednesday 16.00–16.50; office D410.
  • Main textbook: Oppenheim and Willsky, Signals and Systems, 2nd edition, 1997.
  • Supplementary books: Buck–Daniel–Singer, Signals and Systems using MATLAB, 2nd edition, 2002; N. K. Sinha, Linear Systems, 1991.

The grading weights are attendance 3%, quizzes 14%, homework 4%, MATLAB project 4%, midterm exam 35%, and final exam 40%.

Fourier series and transform problems

In the third, fourth, and fifth problems, take c=0, d=1 for S₁ and g=1, h=0 for S₂. a, b or e, f are selected from the table of the second problem.

  • Problem 3: Find the Fourier series of the output for x(t)=cos2πt or x[n]=sin(3πn/4).
  • Problem 4: First find the frequency response H(jω) or H(e^(jω)); then compute the output using the FT/DTFT for x(t)=t e^(−2t)u(t) or x[n]=(−1/2)ⁿu[n].
  • Problem 5: Find the output using the FT/DTFT for x(t)=5e^(−3t)u(t) or x[n]=δ[n]+(1/2)δ[n−1].

In the third and fifth problems, the index i is replaced by i+15 in the first half and by i−15 in the second half. The last three problems are due on the Friday of the fourteenth week.

2017 project announcement

The announcement states that a MATLAB code similar to each type of problem has been uploaded to the site as an m-file in a ZIP. For Laplace problems 1–4, it recommends the IVP With Laplace Transforms and Inverse Laplace Transforms, Example 3a sections of Lamar University's differential equations pages.

The addresses given in the document are preserved as historical source references:

  • http://tutorial.math.lamar.edu/Classes/DE/IVPWithLaplace.aspx
  • http://tutorial.math.lamar.edu/Classes/DE/InverseTransforms.aspx#Laplace_InvTrans_Ex3a

The six related MATLAB solutions are in the archived post Laplace and Z Transform Homework. This supplement preserves the announcement itself.
