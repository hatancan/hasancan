EEM438 Data Communications: Wireless and Mobile Networks: Second Set of Notes

EEM438 – Veri İletişimi · 17.03.2015 · 18.10.2026

Wireless network elements and medium access

The host, the base station, and the wireless link are the basic elements. Infrastructure and ad hoc networks, as well as single-hop and multi-hop structures, are compared. Attenuation, interference, SNR, BER, and the hidden terminal problem are explained. Passive scanning begins with a beacon, and active scanning begins with a probe. CSMA/CA, DIFS, SIFS, ACK, RTS/CTS, and random backoff are drawn.

Mobility and cellular architecture

Within the same subnet, the access point can change without the IP address changing. As the SNR drops, the error probability increases. Short notes on power management, 802.15, and 802.16 are included, along with diagrams of 2G, 2.5G, and 3G.

The home agent and the foreign agent associate the permanent address with the care-of address. Registration informs the home network of the mobile's location. In indirect routing, the home agent acts as an intermediary; in direct routing, the correspondent learns the care-of address.

Handoff, HLR, and VLR

The HLR is associated with the home network and the VLR with the visited network, through their subscription/location records. The old BSS notifies the MSC of an impending handoff; the new BSS allocates resources and a radio channel. When ready, the mobile switches to the new channel, completion is signaled, and the old resources are released. Although mobility is intended to be transparent to the upper layers, delay and loss can affect performance.

Note: 802.15 is summarized as "the advanced form of Bluetooth"; it is actually a family of standards. The wording in the source was left as is and should not be read as a statement of synonymy.
