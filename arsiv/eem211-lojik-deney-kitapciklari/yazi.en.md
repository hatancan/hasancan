This post presents five Turkish laboratory manuals collected in the folder of the EEM211 Digital Logic Design course, for reference when preparing for the lab. All of them belong to other institutions or websites; the source of each and the experiments it contains are listed below. The five PDFs can be downloaded as a ZIP at the end of the post.

## İTÜ: Digital System Design Applications Experiments (2004)

A 40-page set of lab handouts from the Circuits and Systems Department of the Electronics Engineering Program, Faculty of Electrical and Electronics Engineering, Istanbul Technical University (the PDF is dated August 2004; the file name is "sstu_2005_deneyler"). Nine experiments:

1. TTL and CMOS gate characteristics (input/output levels, propagation delay, fan-out; measurement of TTL and CMOS NAND gates).
1. Implementation of Boolean functions with SSI gate elements and MSI decoders/multiplexers.
1. Combinational circuit synthesis with SSI and MSI elements.
1. Synchronous sequential circuit analysis.
1. Sequence detector.
1. Synchronous sequential circuit synthesis.
1. Asynchronous and synchronous counters.
1. Shift registers.
1. Addition and subtraction circuits with SSI and MSI elements.

## Logic Experiments (2005)

The preparer and institution of this 32-page lab booklet are not stated in the document (the PDF is dated November 2005); the experiments refer to "sections 7, 8 and 10 of the lecture notes". Nine experiments:

1. Logic voltage levels of logic gates (e.g., for a TTL NOT gate, V_IL = 0.8 V, V_IH = 2 V, V_OL = 0.2 V, V_OH = 3.4 V).
1. Design of a code converter and an encoder with gate elements.
1. Parallel adder circuit implemented with SSI elements.
1. Logic circuit applications with MUX and decoder MSI elements.
1. Parallel adder and EPROM LSI integrated circuits.
1. Sequential circuit analysis (analysis of a Mealy machine).
1. Sequential circuit synthesis: a sequence detector that detects the four-bit sequence (0101).
1. Asynchronous counters.
1. An application of a medium-scale integrated circuit element, the register.

## Süleyman Demirel University: Digital Electronics II Lab Reports (2001)

"Logic Circuits II Course Lab Catalogue" (version 2.5.2): a compilation of lab reports prepared in the spring semester of 2001 for the **Digital Electronics II** course at the Department of Electronics and Computer Education, Faculty of Technical Education, Süleyman Demirel University, published on the personal website (SevketKeser.Net) of the student who prepared the reports. Each report includes the purpose of the experiment, the materials used, theoretical background and circuit design, the connection diagram, and the result of the experiment. The experiments are:

- Magnitude comparators (comparing two 2-bit numbers with the 7485).
- Study of a 4x1 multiplexer with the 74151 IC.
- Design of a circuit satisfying given conditions using a J-K flip-flop IC.
- Mod-7 synchronous counter (counting from 0 to 6 with J-K flip-flops).
- Mod-8 asynchronous up/down counter with direct reset.
- Asynchronous counter with reset that counts from 0 to 9 and wraps around, and a presettable asynchronous counter that counts from 4 to 9.
- Johnson counter with D flip-flops.
- Serial-in parallel-out (74164) and parallel-in parallel-out (74174) registers.
- Transistor astable multivibrator and op-amp applications.

## kumanda.org: Automation Workshop Digital Experiments (2007)

"Automation Workshop Lecture Notes – Experiments" (www.kumanda.org), 15 pages. Each experiment lists the components used (IC, 220 Ω resistors, red and green LEDs) and the circuits to be built:

1. AND gate applications – 7408
1. OR gate applications – 7432
1. NOT gate applications – 7404
1. NAND gate applications – 7400
1. NOR gate applications – 7402
1. XOR gate applications – 7486
1. Display applications – CQY91A
1. Decoder applications – 7447 (BCD to 7-segment display decoder)

## MEB MEGEP: Logic Applications 3 (2007)

The "Logic Applications 3" module (Ankara 2007, module code 523EO0018, 77 pages), prepared for the Electrical and Electronics Technology field under the Strengthening Vocational Education and Training System Project (MEGEP) of the Ministry of National Education. The modules are stated to be freely accessible online and not to be used for commercial purposes. It has three learning activities:

1. **Counters:** asynchronous up and down counters, synchronous up and down counters, modulo counters.
1. **Registers:** serial-in serial-out, serial-in parallel-out, parallel-in serial-out, and parallel-in parallel-out registers.
1. **Digital-to-analog and analog-to-digital converters:** fundamentals of D/A converters, the basic R-2R ladder D/A converter, D/A ICs; fundamentals of A/D converters, the parallel-comparator (flash) A/D converter, A/D ICs.

Each activity ends with an application activity, measurement and evaluation questions, and a performance test; answer keys are provided at the end of the module.
