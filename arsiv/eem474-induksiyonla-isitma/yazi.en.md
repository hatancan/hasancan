## Operating principle

Induction heating is the contactless heating of electrically conductive metals and carbon-based materials. Electromagnetic energy is converted into thermal energy. A time-varying magnetic field induces a voltage in the conductor; the resulting eddy currents release heat through I²R losses due to resistance.

The presentation covers Faraday's and Lenz's laws together. Lenz's law states that the induced current acts in the direction opposing the change in the magnetic flux that produces it.

## System components

1. AC power supply: Referred to as an inverter or induction generator. It converts mains energy into high-frequency energy. The presentation gives ranges of 1–400 kHz and 2–500 kW.
2. Inductor coil: Transfers the energy it receives from the power supply to the workpiece through the magnetic field.
3. Workpiece to be heated: The part in which eddy currents are generated in the field created by the coil.

## Applications

The processing of materials such as those in the automotive, aerospace, general industry, and steel sectors is given as examples.
