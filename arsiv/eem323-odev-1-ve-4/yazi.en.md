Two homework sheets given by Prof. Dr. S. Gökhun Tanyer in the **EEM323 Electromagnetics II** course: Homework 1 (September 23, 2014) and Homework 4 (October 20, 2016). The original PDFs are in the ZIP file at the end of the post.

## Homework 1 – Introduction to the course

It is stated that doing the homework without help from anyone will directly improve exam performance, that small mistakes will not be penalized, and that homework submitted after the first lecture will incur a 20% penalty, with an additional 20% penalty for each extra day.

1. Start-of-term preparation:
    - If you had the opportunity to become a researcher in any field after graduation, which topics would you want to work on and which problems would you want to solve?
    - In a table, list the topics you struggle with and would benefit from reviewing, especially in EEM224 and EEM323.
    - Next to each topic, write the problem that challenges you and its estimated source.
2. Piano research:
    - How many keys does a grand piano have?
    - Find the Do, Re, Mi equivalents of the keys labeled with the pitch names A, B, C.
    - If the lowest-pitched (leftmost) key is A0, find which key number each of the keys in the series C1, C2, C3, …, Ca is and their frequencies; for the highest-pitched Ca key, what is a, and what is its frequency?
    - Draw a two-dimensional graph with C1, C2, C3, …, equally spaced on the x-axis and the frequency of each key on the y-axis; connect the points and explain in your own words whether the graph is linear.

## Homework 4 – Application of Faraday's law

Homework must include the homework number, name, and student number; the software for any computer outputs must also be provided; solutions other than software must be submitted handwritten, stapled, without a cover page, and without a plastic sleeve; for late submissions there is a 20% penalty per day.

- **1A.** Prepare your résumé as if applying to an employer and attach a copy.
- **1B.** Open accounts on LinkedIn and ResearchGate, fill in your personal information, and attach a printout.
- **1C.** Download at least two articles on a topic you are curious about from the Web of Science database (www.webofscience.com), and attach at least two that you made use of.
- **1D. Research assignment:** Find S. G. Tanyer's voice activity detection paper published in 2000; find how many citations it has received in WoS and in Google Scholar (Scopus for those who wish); download the article "A new emergent pursuit in music: the ultimate solution to Al-Jurjani's 1,000-year-old signal processing problem" and find out who Al-Jurjani was.
- **Question 2:** Faraday's law of electric potential generation (induction): (2A) theoretical calculation, (2B) developing software from this calculation and obtaining the outputs. (The figure on the sheet cannot be rendered as text; see the original PDF.)
