EEM311 Electronics II: Laboratory Information Booklet, Rules, Grading and Experiment Schedule

EEM311 – Elektronik II · 06.10.2010 · 16.10.2026

This post contains the EEM311 Electronics II Laboratory Information Booklet of the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, Başkent University. The version used as the basis is the fall 2010 booklet (6 October 2010); the 2007–2008 summer term version of the booklet was also in the folder, and the differences between the two versions are noted separately. The booklet was prepared by the laboratory assistants. The original PDFs of both versions can be downloaded as a ZIP at the end of the post.

How the laboratory works

  1. The laboratory pre-lab assignments are obtained from the relevant web page one week before the laboratory day, and students submit their pre-lab on the laboratory day. Students who come without having completed their pre-lab cannot do the laboratory work.
  2. Each student will perform the experiments individually. Group work will not be permitted except in cases of necessity.
  3. The student is responsible for the proper functioning of the materials or the experiment set used. The student is responsible for anything that may affect the experimental result during the work (such as a short circuit on the breadboard).
  4. At the end of the experimental work, students must show the completed work to the assistant on duty. The assistant on duty records each student's completed work; if necessary, they may ask the student questions about the work or ask them to make changes to it.
  5. At the end of the experimental work, each student submits their pre-lab and the documents and materials related to the experimental work to the assistant on duty, and tidies up the laboratory equipment and the experiment bench.
  6. Experiment reports are submitted, provided that they comply with the given format, no later than the next experiment. For reports submitted late, the following penalty is applied to that week's experiment grade:
DelayPenalty
1 day−10%
2 days−20%
3 days−30%
4 days or moreLaboratory grade is zero

Important

  • There is no make-up right for experiments that are missed without an official excuse.
  • A make-up right will be given for 1 experiment that could not be performed due to an official health excuse (an official doctor's report). Students who miss 2 experiments without an official excuse will be considered to have failed the course.
  • Students who cannot obtain at least 25% of the full grade from the laboratory will be considered to have received an F for the entire course.

Note: In the booklet this item is written as "A make-up right will be given for 1 experiment not performed without an official health excuse (official doctor's report)"; when read together with the previous item, what is meant is "1 experiment that could not be performed, with a report".

General rules

  1. Walking around inside the laboratory and leaving without permission during the experiment is prohibited.
  2. Eating and drinking anything in the laboratory is prohibited.
  3. Each student is responsible for the experiment set and materials they use.
  4. Students who do not return the laboratory materials at the end of the experiment or who leave without tidying the experiment bench have their laboratory grade for that week reduced by 5%.

Pre-lab and report format

  • Pre-labs and reports must have the attached cover page; pre-labs and reports without a cover are not evaluated.
  • Except for work uploaded to the web page, all pre-labs and experiment reports are submitted written on white A4 paper. Reports and pre-labs written on notebook pages, scratch paper, or lined or squared paper are strictly not accepted.
  • What is requested in the pre-lab and report is written out clearly, item by item.
  • The total number of pages and the page number must be indicated on every page.
  • Pre-labs and reports are stapled with a wire stapler; they are not submitted in a plastic sleeve. The student is responsible for the loss of work that is not stapled.
  • The 2007–2008 version additionally had the item "Pre-labs and reports shall be prepared in English".

WARNING: Students who cheat or let others cheat in any way will receive zero for the relevant experiment, and the relevant disciplinary regulation will be applied.
