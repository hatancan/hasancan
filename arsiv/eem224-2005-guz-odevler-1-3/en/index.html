The original course code in the documents is EEM323; the content was archived under EEM224 in accordance with the source folder.

Homework 1: Motional Induction and Displacement Current

A rod of length l on rails has velocity v = âx v₀ cos(ω₁t), and the magnetic field is B = −âz B₀ sin(ω₂t). The position is written as x(t) = x₀ + (v₀/ω₁)sin(ω₁t), and the flux as Φ = −B₀ l x(t)sin(ω₂t). Depending on the chosen loop direction, the voltage is computed from the time derivative of the flux, and the resistor current from I = V/R.

In the second problem, a parallel-plate capacitor with A = 2 cm², d = 1.4 mm, εr = 7 is driven by V(t) = 10 cos(2πft). The displacement current is requested for f = 20 kHz and 4 MHz. C = εA/d ≈ 8.85 pF and I = C dV/dt. Source result: I = −20πf ε₀ sin(2πft); here the numerical value of C comes out the same as ε₀.
