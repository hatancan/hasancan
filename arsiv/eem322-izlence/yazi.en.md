## Homework and MATLAB project (EEM322 HW)

The homework questions were selected from the B-problems at the end of Chapters 5, 6, and 8 of Ogata's *Modern Control Engineering* and from Chapters 5 and 6 of Uraz's *Control Systems*. Each student was given an "HW No," and the problems in the row for that number made up that student's assignment. The columns of the table and the page ranges in the books are as follows:

- **B-5**: Ogata, Chapter 5 B-problems (pp. 330–336)
- **B-6**: Ogata, Chapter 6 B-problems (pp. 413–415)
- **B-6 / Uraz**: Relevant problems from Uraz's book (pp. 229–230)
- **B-8**: Ogata, Chapter 8 B-problems (pp. 612–617)
- **B-8 / Uraz**: Relevant problems from Uraz's book (p. 231)

Submission rules:

- The B-5 and B-6 homework is due by Friday of week 11.
- The B-8 homework is due by Friday of week 14.
- All time responses, root locus plots, Bode plots, and Nyquist diagrams must be drawn **by hand**.

**MATLAB project:** For every step of the solutions in the B-6, B-6/Uraz, and B-8/Uraz homework (wherever applicable), MATLAB code must be provided; MATLAB plots of all time responses, root loci, Bode plots, and Nyquist diagrams of the systems examined must be included. Due: Friday of week 14.
