EEM226 Electromagnetic Wave Theory I: Course Content Form (2016)

EEM226 – Elektromanyetik Dalga Teorisi I · 17.02.2016 · 16.10.2026

This post is the course content form for the Başkent University Electrical and Electronics Engineering course EEM 226 (abbreviated DIF in the file name). The form was prepared and signed on 17 February 2016 by the course instructor, Prof. Dr. S. Gökhun Tanyer. The form gives the course name as "Elektromanyetik – I" (Electromagnetics I); in the same instructor's course plan and introductory presentation, however, the course is titled "Electromagnetic Wave Theory I". The original PDF of the two-page form can be downloaded at the end of the post.

Other topics

Do not lose this document; keep it. Understanding the underlined topics in particular will certainly benefit you throughout the course.

Lectures

  • Your EEM226 course is one of the most fundamental courses in your EE engineering education. Courses directly related after EEM226: EEM323, EEM423, EEM424, EEM538, EEM549, ...
  • Over the last 15 years, it has been observed that students who have gaps in class attendance, who do not review after lectures, and who do not take their homework seriously fail in the long run in the vector algebra topic that we will cover within the first month of this semester.

Projects

  • You will gain experience in developing modeling software, which cannot be offered as a course.
  • There will be no interim submission. The project will be submitted at the beginning of the first class in week 12.
  • Project grading: project submission format 15%, theory 20%, software (code) text 20%, figures, comments, and results 20%, CD/DVD output 20%, references 5%.

Homework

  • Homework questions: Keep a record of the homework questions given during classes throughout the week. Write them down; do not lose them. Submit the questions given during the week at the start of the first class of the following week.
  • Submission date and time: All homework submitted after the class has started will be considered late. Late homework will be graded with a 50% penalty. Homework that is more than one week late will not be accepted.
  • Prepare all explanations in your own handwriting, drawing figures when necessary.
  • Do not use a cover page or plastic sleeve folders for your homework.
  • To prevent the problems experienced in the past from recurring, homework that is missing or has incorrect information on the first page (first name/surname/course name/homework number/submission date) will not be accepted. A 5% grade deduction will be applied for each missing item.
  • Homework cannot be submitted to the course assistant.

Quizzes, midterm exam, final exam

  • Fill in your first name, surname, and student number completely on every page.
  • Do not ask the exam proctors questions. State all units in your results (if any).
  • Read all questions carefully and understand them fully before starting the solution.
  • Provide vectorial notation in your answers completely.
  • Detail integrals (if any) to the level of defining their limits and variables.
  • Results without a solution will not be graded. Do not provide information beyond the method required for the solution.
  • Write the answer to each question outside the question area, in the separate spaces provided, simplified and enclosed in a box.
  • In your solutions, show scalars and vectors in a way that they cannot be confused.
  • Before writing your result, always give the solution and provide your reasoning. Answers without a solution will not be graded.
  • State all units of the answers. Units are graded separately. Enclose all your answers in a box.
  • You may visit my office to go over your questions, in groups if needed, after preparing for them beforehand. We will go into the necessary detail at the board in my office and solve them together. I especially invite those who hesitate to ask questions in class. However, please do not wait until the last week to ask questions. I would like to state in advance that I will not be able to help with your questions after I have prepared the exam questions.
  • I invite anyone who thinks they have not been successful in the homework, quiz, and midterm results to my office. Let us review the topics one-on-one. I wish you success in all your exams in advance.
  • Without waiting until the end of the semester, you can send me directly your opinions and suggested solutions to any possible problems regarding this course. (Dr. S. Gökhun Tanyer, A-404.)

Course Moodle page

  • Access to the site by unauthorized persons has been blocked. The password for our site will be given to you in class.
  • You can visit our site for all course documents to be shared, such as DIF and HDPD.
  • Your exam results will be sent to the e-mail address you registered on Moodle as soon as they are determined. Since there will be no delay, there is no opportunity to learn them earlier by visiting my office.
  • You can download and examine some of the videos / animations related to the course and covered in class from our site.
  • If the documents shared on our site conflict with the information presented during class, the information on this site is not valid, as it consists of older versions. The most up-to-date and valid information is given during class.
  • Files that cannot be uploaded to this platform due to file size will be provided in class to students who request them.

I wish you a good semester and hope you gain lasting knowledge that you will certainly use in your engineering career. With my greetings and affection. — Prof. Dr. Süleyman Gökhun Tanyer
