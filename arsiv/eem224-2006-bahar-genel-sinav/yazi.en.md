## Circular current and charge
In the xy plane, a current loop of radius b=3 m carries I=250/9 A. A charge loop of radius c=4 m, centered at z=c+b, has a density of 250πε₀/3 C/m. B and E are asked at (0,0,c). The Biot–Savart axis integral gives B=μ₀âz; the charge loop gives E=−4πâz V/m. Since the velocity of the q=1 C charge is parallel to the field, v×B=0, and F=−4πâz N.

*Note: In the question sheet the velocity is given as −4πâz, whereas in the solution it is written as 10⁷âz; since both velocities are parallel to B, the force result of this problem does not change. The unit of B is written as "Weber" in the solution; the unit of flux density is the tesla.*

## Dielectric boundary
z<0: ε₁=2ε₀; z>0: ε₂=4ε₀. E₁=−3âx+4ây−2âz. D₁=ε₀(−6âx+8ây−4âz), P₁=ε₀(−3âx+4ây−2âz). E₂=−3âx+4ây−âz, D₂=ε₀(−12âx+16ây−4âz), P₂=ε₀(−9âx+12ây−3âz). The total polarization surface charge is ε₀.

## Magnetic coaxial shell
The inner shell at r=1 carries current I, and the outer shell at r=2 carries current −I (opposite direction); between them μ=2μ₀r. The field is zero in the inner and outer regions; for 1<r<2, H=Iâφ/(2πr), B=μ₀Iâφ/π. M=(2r−1)Iâφ/(2πr), Jm=Iâz/(πr). The equivalent volume current is 2I, the inner surface current is I, and the outer surface current is −3I; the total is zero.

At r=1 the pressure is μ₀I²/(4π²), in the −r direction. The energy per unit length is μ₀I²/(2π), and the inductance is μ₀/π.

## Cylindrical capacitor with two dielectrics
a=2, b=5, c=6 m; L=5 m. For 2<r<5, ε₁=20ε₀/r, and for 5<r<6, ε₂=10ε₀/r; V₀=1/2 V. D=Kâr/r, and from the voltage integral K=2ε₀. E₁=âr/10, E₂=âr/5; the field is zero inside the conductors and in the outer region. Q=20πε₀, C=40πε₀ F. The field-energy integral and ½CV² give the same result: 5πε₀ J.
