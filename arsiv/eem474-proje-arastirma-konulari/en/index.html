EEM474 Power Electronics: Project and Research Topics

EEM474 – Güç Elektroniği · 05.05.2015 · 18.10.2026

Project announcements and topic lists from the EEM474 archive have been compiled, with student names and assignment schedules removed. The 5 May 2015 update is included, and older lists with no stated year are given below in separate sections. This post contains only the topic texts; the personnel lists cannot be downloaded.

2015 research and presentation topics

The announcement asks for two preferences to be submitted, allows the presentation and the simulation to be chosen from the same topic, and requires the work to be handed in during the final week. The presentation is graded out of 7 points and the simulation out of 5.

  1. Flyback converter.
  2. Zero current switching converter.
  3. Zero voltage switching converter.
  4. SEPIC converter.
  5. Ćuk converter.
  6. Matrix converter.
  7. Ultrasonic motors.
  8. Forward converter.
  9. Multilevel converters and inverters.
  10. Space vector inverter.
  11. Synchronous buck converter.
  12. Phase-shifted full-bridge DC/DC converter.
  13. Comparison of V/Hz and field oriented control in motor control.
  14. Interleaved/multiphase buck converter.
  15. Operating principle and analysis of the forward converter.
  16. Two-switch forward converter.
  17. Push-pull converter.
  18. Switched-capacitor converters.
  19. Power factor correction with a DC/DC converter.
  20. Series resonant DC/DC converter.

2015 design and simulation topics

A PSIM or MATLAB simulation is required, with the input and output voltages and component values to be selected.

  1. Series regulator and its performance under different loads.
  2. Shunt regulator and its performance under different loads.
  3. Ćuk converter.
  4. SEPIC converter.
  5. Single-phase cycloconverter.
  6. Boost with voltage feedback.
  7. Buck-boost with voltage feedback.
  8. Fully controlled single-phase AC/DC with zero-crossing circuit.
  9. Half-controlled single-phase AC/DC with zero-crossing circuit.
  10. ON/OFF controlled AC/AC.
  11. Flyback converter.
  12. Forward converter.
  13. Two-switch forward.
  14. Push-pull.
  15. Flyback design and analysis (appears a second time in the list).
  16. Series resonant inverter.
  17. Series resonant DC/DC.
  18. Synchronous buck.
  19. Phase-shifted full-bridge DC/DC.
  20. Average current mode control on a buck converter.

  4. Push-pull research and simulation.
  5. Use of PLL in power electronics.
  6. PWM rectifier.

  10. Phase-controlled rectifier with voltage feedback.
  11. Peak current mode control on a buck converter.
  12. Space vector inverter.
  13. Half-bridge DC/AC inverter with dead time generator.
  14. SEPIC calculation and simulation.
  15. Ćuk calculation and simulation.
  16. Buck-boost calculation and simulation.
  17. Multilevel inverter.
  18. Single-phase quasi-square inverter and waveform generator.
  19. Three-phase square-wave inverter and waveform generator.
  20. ON/OFF controlled AC/AC voltage converter.

Old research list

Electric vehicles; dynamic voltage restorer (DVR); HVDC transmission; maximum power point tracking; solar cells; soft switching; ZVS/ZCS; software such as MATLAB, Simulink and PSpice; PFC; SEPIC; ballast; Ćuk; PWM rectifier; matrix converter; induction heating; ultrasonic motor; piezoelectric motor; wind energy; PaveGen; flyback; resonant converters; cogeneration; intelligent power module; synchronous motor and generator; linear motor; direct drive motor; power quality; multilevel converters and inverters; current sensors; piezoelectric transformers and converters. The list also states that students may propose new topics.

Microcontroller topics from the same workbooks

DC motor direction/speed control with the L298; unipolar and bipolar stepper motor; RC servo; speed calculation from encoder pulses; linearizing Sharp sensor data with a lookup table or fuzzy logic; pulse width and frequency measurement with CCP; three-position and dead-band control; sine generation with PWM; serial ADC and LCD; fuzzy logic robot; CAN/USB; dishwasher simulation with C and Proteus; saving to EEPROM. The last EEPROM line is incomplete in the source.

Note: The older schedules contain May updates but no year. They were not merged as if they belonged to the same period as the 2015 list. The announcements are archive documents; they are not a current call for submissions or preferences.
