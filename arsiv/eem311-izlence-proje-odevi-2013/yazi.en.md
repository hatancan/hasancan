## Project assignment

**Deadline:** 03.01.2014 (it may also be submitted earlier).

**Topic:** Each student will choose an electronic circuit at a scale they can manage, carry out all stages of building the circuit themselves, and prepare a report about their circuit.

### Rules for the assignment

- The circuit that is the subject of the assignment must serve a purpose of your choosing.
- After completing the circuit, you must show it to your course instructor or the teaching assistants.
- Since each student is required to build a different circuit, register the name of your circuit with the course instructor.
- The assignment will be graded out of 100 points and 7% of it will be added to the course grade: circuit 65%, report 35%.
- The content of the report must be as specified below.
- Assignments submitted late will absolutely not be accepted.

### Rules for the report

- Reports will be prepared on A4 paper, using a computer.
- No cover page should be made; the student number should be written at the top left of the first page.
- The purpose for which the circuit was built, that is, what it is used for, will be explained.
- The schematic of the circuit (drawn by yourself) will be provided, and its operation, that is, what each component in the circuit does, will be explained.
- The printed circuit board (PCB) layout of the circuit will be provided.
- At least one photograph of the final version of the circuit will be included.
- The stages of building the circuit will be described in order.
- What you learned while building the circuit and the difficulties you encountered will be stated.
- The datasheets of the important components in the circuit will be provided in an appendix.
- References will be added, if any.
