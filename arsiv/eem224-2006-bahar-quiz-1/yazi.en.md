## Divergence in the unit cube
F=(x+2)âx−2yâz, 0≤x,y,z≤1. ∇·F=1; the volume integral is 1. The contributions of the x=0 and x=1 surfaces are −2 and 3; the contributions of the z=0 and z=1 surfaces are 1 and −1. The y surfaces are zero; the total is 1.

## Stokes on the unit square
The contour in the xy plane is (0,0)→(1,0)→(1,1)→(0,1), and F=2xyâx−yâz. ∇×F=−âx−2xâz. The surface integral with the +z normal direction is −1. The line integrals of the four edges are 0, 0, −1, 0; the total is −1, and Stokes' theorem is satisfied.
