This post brings together the course content forms of the Başkent University Electrical and Electronics Engineering **Signals and Systems II** course for three different years. The course was offered under the code **EEM 302** in 2008-2009 and under the code **EEM 308** from 2009-2010 onward. The first two forms were prepared by the course's instructor at the time, **Assoc. Prof. Dr. İsmail Avcıbaş**, and the 2012-2013 form by **Dr. Murat Üçüncü** (12 February 2013). Alongside them is the question list of the first homework (HW 1) given in the spring of 2009. The originals of the four documents can be downloaded as a ZIP at the end of the post.

## Weekly Topics

| Week | 2008-2009 (EEM 302) | 2009-2010 (EEM 308) | 2012-2013 (EEM 308) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Laplace transform | Laplace transform | Laplace transform |
| 2 | Laplace transform | Laplace transform | Laplace transform |
| 3 | Z-transform | Time and frequency analysis of continuous-time LTI systems | z-transform |
| 4 | Z-transform | Z-transform | z-transform |
| 5 | Z-transform | Z-transform | Time response of LTI (linear time-invariant) systems |
| 6 | Time and frequency analysis of LTI systems | Z-transform | Frequency response of LTI systems |
| 7 | Time and frequency analysis of LTI systems | Time and frequency analysis of discrete-time LTI systems | Frequency response of LTI systems |
| 8 | Midterm exam | Midterm exam | Midterm exam |
| 9 | Sampling theory and applications | Sampling theory and applications | Transfer functions of systems |
| 10 | Sampling theory and applications | Sampling theory and applications | State-space descriptions of systems |
| 11 | Sampling theory and applications | Response of linear systems to random inputs | Bode diagrams |
| 12 | Introduction to filter theory | Response of linear systems to random inputs | Sampling theory |
| 13 | Communication systems | Communication systems | Filter design |
| 14 | Communication systems | Communication systems | Filter design |

The common backbone of the three plans is the same: first the **Laplace transform** in continuous time, then the **z-transform** in discrete time, followed by time and frequency analysis of linear time-invariant (LTI) systems and, in the second half of the semester, **sampling theory**. The 2009 plan features communication systems (amplitude modulation), the 2010 plan additionally covers random inputs, and the 2013 plan highlights state-space representation, Bode diagrams and filter design.
