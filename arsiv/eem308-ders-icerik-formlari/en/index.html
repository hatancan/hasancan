EEM308 Signals and Systems II: Course Content Forms and Weekly Topics (2009–2013)

EEM308 – Sinyaller ve Sistemler II · 15.02.2010 · 16.10.2026

This post brings together the course content forms of the Başkent University Electrical and Electronics Engineering Signals and Systems II course for three different years. The course was offered under the code EEM 302 in 2008-2009 and under the code EEM 308 from 2009-2010 onward. The first two forms were prepared by the course's instructor at the time, Assoc. Prof. Dr. İsmail Avcıbaş, and the 2012-2013 form by Dr. Murat Üçüncü (12 February 2013). Alongside them is the question list of the first homework (HW 1) given in the spring of 2009. The originals of the four documents can be downloaded as a ZIP at the end of the post.

The 2012-2013 form also includes the following note: students may also meet with the instructor outside the stated hours, at a time agreed upon in advance. The warning on the same form: students who fail due to absenteeism receive an F2 grade.

Textbook and References

  • Textbook: Alan V. Oppenheim, Alan S. Willsky, S. Hamid Nawab, Signals & Systems, Prentice Hall, 1997, second edition (the same in all three forms).
  • Additional reference (2009, 2010): John Buck, Michael Daniel, Andrew Singer, Computer Explorations in Signals and Systems Using MATLAB, Prentice Hall, 2001, second edition.

The common backbone of the three plans is the same: first the Laplace transform in continuous time, then the z-transform in discrete time, followed by time and frequency analysis of linear time-invariant (LTI) systems and, in the second half of the semester, sampling theory. The 2009 plan features communication systems (amplitude modulation), the 2010 plan additionally covers random inputs, and the 2013 plan highlights state-space representation, Bode diagrams and filter design.

EEM 302 HW 1 (2009)

The first homework from the textbook, given in the spring of 2009; the due date was 8 May 2009:

  • Chapter 8 (Communication systems) problems: 8.3, 8.4, 8.6, 8.10, 8.14
  • Chapter 10 (z-transform) problems: 10.2, 10.4, 10.9, 10.16, 10.27, 10.33

The problem numbers refer to the second edition of Oppenheim & Willsky.
