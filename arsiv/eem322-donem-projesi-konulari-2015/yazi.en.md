This post is the **term project announcement** for the 2015 spring term of the **EEM322 Control Systems** course at Başkent University. The document was prepared by the course instructor **Assoc. Prof. Mustafa Doğan** on 11.03.2015; in the document the course code appears as "EMM 322". The two-page announcement lists the topics of the project groups and the sections expected in the project report. Student names in the group list have been removed; the masked PDF can be downloaded at the end of the post.

**Report/Code+Experiment/Presentation due date:** 15 May 2015.

## Sample report contents

On the second page of the announcement, a template is given for the table of contents of the project report:

1. Introduction (problem definition, design criteria, level of difficulty)
1. Acknowledgments / Thanks
1. Symbols and abbreviations
1. System modeling
1. Model validation / comparison
1. Uncertainty and disturbance analysis
1. Simulation results (MATLAB / Simulink / SimMechanics): outputs, comments
1. Discussion and conclusions
1. Appendices: commented MATLAB files

In the template, the sections **Introduction**, **Model validation/comparison**, **Uncertainty and disturbance analysis**, and **Discussion and conclusions** are emphasized with large, underlined text.

> Apart from the report, any programs written will be run and tested as MATLAB files on the presentation day, and their contents, with every line commented, are to be included in the report.
