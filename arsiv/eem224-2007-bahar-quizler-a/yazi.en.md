## Quiz 2 A: charges
On the upper half circle of radius b=2 m, ρl=ρ₀sinφ, ρ₀=−16πε₀, 0≤φ≤π. At the center, E=πây, V=−8 V. The work of bringing a 10 C charge from infinity to the center is −80 J.

A −1 μC charge is at (2,−1,4) and a 3 μC charge is at (−1,−1,3). E and V are asked at (2,−1,3). The solution finds E=[10⁻⁶/(4πε₀)](âz+âx/3), V=0. Below the handout, another two-charge question is printed under the same "S.2" number: 0.4 μC and −12 μC; positions (−4,2,−8), (2,2,−6), observation point (−4,2,−6). There is no separate solution for this additional line.

## Test 3 A: conducting dielectric
For 0.25<y<0.5: σ=2/y, ε=ε₀/y; V₀=3, S=1/8. J=64ây, E=32yây, D=32ε₀ây, P=32ε₀(1−y)ây. ρpv=32ε₀. Volume charge ε₀, total on the lower surface −3ε₀, total on the upper surface 2ε₀; net zero. I=8 A, R=3/8 Ω, the magnitude of the free charge on the upper surface is 4ε₀, C=4ε₀/3, energy 6ε₀ J. At y=0.5 the pressure is 256ε₀ N/m², in the +y direction.

## Test 4 A: conductor boundary and magnetic field
x<0: ε₁=4ε₀, σ₁=2; x>0: ε₂=3ε₀, σ₂=1. J₁=4âx+3ây. E₁=2âx+1.5ây, E₂=4âx+1.5ây, J₂=4âx+1.5ây. D₁=ε₀(8âx+6ây), D₂=ε₀(12âx+4.5ây); P₁=ε₀(6âx+4.5ây), P₂=ε₀(8âx+3ây). Polarization surface charge −2ε₀.

*Note: In the solution, the y components were subtracted for the free surface charge and −1.5ε₀ was written. Since the boundary is x=0, the normal x components should be used; the correct difference is 4ε₀.*

A wire parallel to z and a cylinder with an axis spacing of 5 m, cylinder radius 1 m; wire 6π A, cylinder J=2âz. At y=3, the cylinder contribution is μ₀âx/2, the wire contribution is −μ₀âx; the total is −μ₀âx/2.
