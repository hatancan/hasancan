The questions of three midterm exams in the probability courses taught by Lecturer Pelin Toktaş at Başkent University. The questions were transcribed to text from photographed exam papers; the photographs themselves are not published. (Passages that could not be read because of handwritten notes and stains on some papers are marked with square brackets.) The solutions are not on the papers.

## MAT 340 Probability Midterm Exam – 27 November 2004 (2004-2005 fall)

**Question 1 (15 points).** A bag contains 5 coins. Of these 5 coins, 3 are fair (the probability of heads equals the probability of tails), and 2 are biased coins with a probability of getting tails of [illegible]. In the experiment, a coin is drawn at random from this bag and tossed [once]. (a) (7 points) Calculate the probability of getting tails as the result of the experiment. (b) (8 points) Given that heads came up, find the probability that the coin is biased.

**Question 2 (16 points).** The random variable X has the following cumulative distribution function: F(x) = 0 (x < 0); 1/2 (0 ≤ x < 1); 3/5 (1 ≤ x < 2); 4/5 (2 ≤ x < 3); 9/10 (3 ≤ x < 3.5); 1 (x ≥ 3.5). (a) (7 points) Find the probability mass function of X. (b) (5 points) If Y = −2X + 1, find the expected value of Y. (c) (4 points) P(0.25 ≤ X < 3) = ?

**Question 3 (22 points).** The random variable X has the probability mass function p(x) = (3x − 1)/15, x = 1, 2, 3 (0 elsewhere). (a) (7 points) Find the moment generating function of X. (b) (7 points) Using the moment generating function, calculate the expected value and the variance of X. (c) (8 points) Y is defined as Y = (3X − 1)/2; find the probability mass function of Y.

**Question 4 (17 points).** A company pays its employees an average of $15.90 per hour with a standard deviation of $1.5. If the distribution of wages is normal, (a) (8 points) what percentage of the employees in the company earn between $13.75 and $16.22 per hour? (b) (9 points) Find the hourly wage above which the highest-paid 5% of the employees in the company are paid.

**Question 5 (30 points).** The random variables X and Y have the joint probability density function f(x, y) = c·x, 1 ≤ x ≤ y ≤ 3 (0 elsewhere). (a) (6 points) Find the value of the constant "c". (b) (7 points) Are the random variables X and Y independent? Show. (c) (5 points) Find the cumulative distribution function of the random variable X. (d) (5 points) Find the expected value of the random variable Y. (e) (7 points) P(Y < 2.5 | X = 2) = ?

A table of areas under the standard normal curve is attached to the paper.

## END 204 Introduction to Probability and Statistics Midterm Exam – 10 April 2007 (Industrial Engineering, 2006-2007 spring)

**Question 1 (16 points).** It is known that the CDs produced by a company are defective independently of one another with probability 0.05. The company produces CDs in packs of ten, and when two or more CDs in a pack turn out to be defective, the pack is taken back by the company and the customer is refunded. (a) (6 points) What is the probability that no CD in a pack is defective? (b) (10 points) What is the probability that a person who buys 3 packs of CDs returns one pack and gets a refund?

**Question 2 (16 points).** The first box contains 6 red and 4 green balls; the second box contains 7 red and 3 green balls. A ball is selected at random from the first box and put into the second box. Then a ball is drawn from the second box and put into the first box. (a) (4 points) What is the probability that the ball selected from the first box is red and the ball selected from the second box is red? (b) (5 points) After the drawing-and-placing operations are completed, what is the probability that the numbers of red and green balls in the first box are the same as at the start? (c) (7 points) Given that the ball put from the second box into the first box is green, what is the probability that the ball put from the first box into the second box is red?

**Question 3 (28 points).** The random variable X has the probability density function f(x) = x/25 (0 ≤ x < 5), 2/5 − x/25 (5 ≤ x < 10), 0 (otherwise). (a) (9 points) Find the cumulative distribution function of X. (b) (6 points) Calculate E(−3X − 1). (c) (8 points) Calculate Var(−2X − 2). (d) (5 points) P(1 < X ≤ 6) = ?

**Question 4 (16 points).** The joint probability distribution of X and Y is given as f(x, y) = 6x, 0 < x < 1, 0 < y < 1 − x (0 elsewhere). (a) (6 points) Find the marginal distribution of X. (b) (10 points) P(X > 0.3 | Y = 0.5) = ?

**Question 5 (24 points).** An insurance agent has two appointments to sell individual retirement insurance. The probability of insuring the person in the first meeting is 0.3, and the probability of insuring the person in the second meeting is 0.6. These meetings are assumed to be held independently of each other. The agent has two types of insurance packages, worth 50 YTL and 100 YTL, which can be offered to people and sold with equal probabilities. The random variable X is defined as the total amount of money the agent can earn from these appointments. (a) (15 points) Find the probability mass function of X. (b) (9 points) Find the standard deviation of the total amount of money the agent can earn from these appointments.

## MAT 340 Probability Midterm Exam – 21 November 2007 (2007-2008 fall)

Duration 110 minutes; books and notes are not allowed, and everyone uses their own calculator. Points: Question 1 = 22, Question 2 = 28, Question 3 = 30, Question 4 = 20. At the top of the paper, the formulas of the discrete uniform, Bernoulli, binomial and hypergeometric distributions are given.

**Question 1 (22 points).** A nuclear power plant consists of 3 systems, A, B and C. If systems A and B, or systems A and C, fail, the plant is shut down. Systems A, B and C fail independently of one another with probabilities 0.01, 0.03 and 0.02, respectively. (a) (8 points) What is the probability that the plant is operating? (b) (7 points) Given that system A is not working, what is the probability that the plant is operating? (c) (7 points) Given that the plant is reported to be not operating, what is the probability that system B is working?

**Question 2 (28 points).** The probability density function of the random variable X is given as f(x) = x/4 (0 < x ≤ 2), 3 − x (2 < x ≤ 3), 0 (otherwise). (a) (8 points) P(1 < X ≤ 5/2) = ? (b) (10 points) Find the cumulative distribution function of X. (c) (8 points) Find the expected value of X. (d) (2 points) P(X = 2) = ?

**Question 3 (30 points).** On any given day, at the start of the day the fuel tank contains a random amount Y (in 1000 liters) of kerosene. A random amount X of this is sold during the day. It is assumed that the tank is not refilled during the day. The joint probability distribution of the random variables X and Y is given by f(x, y) = c, 0 < x < y, 0 < y < 1 (0 elsewhere). (a) (6 points) Find the value of the constant "c". (b) (9 points) Are the random variables X and Y independent? Show. (c) (8 points) P(1/4 < X < 1/2 | Y = 3/4) = ? (d) (7 points) What is the expected amount of kerosene remaining in the tank at the end of the day?

**Question 4 (20 points).** A binary code (0 or 1) is used in a communication system. Because of noise entering the system, the data transmitted from the transmitter to the receiver is transmitted incorrectly, independently of one another, with probability 0.2. Suppose that an important single-digit message is to be sent using the communication device. To reduce the chance of error, 00000 is sent instead of 0 and 11111 instead of 1. The receiver decodes the received code based on majority. For example, if the receiver receives the data 01001, the data will be interpreted as 0 because 0 is in the majority. (a) (10 points) What is the probability that this important message is received incorrectly? (b) (10 points) What is the probability that 2 of 4 important messages sent independently of one another are received incorrectly?
