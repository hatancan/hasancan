## Homework 4: Wave Parameters
For E = 2âx cos(10⁸t−z/√3) − ây sin(10⁸t−z/√3) V/m, the source finds f = 10⁸/(2π), λ = 2√3π m, εr = 3 and η = 40√3π Ω. The wave propagates in the +z direction and its polarization is elliptical. H = [âx sin(10⁸t−z/√3) + 2ây cos(10⁸t−z/√3)]/(40√3π).

In a medium with f = 3 GHz, εr = 2.5, μ = μ₀ and a loss tangent of 10⁻², a y-polarized wave propagates in the +x direction. At x=0, E = ây 50 sin(6π×10⁹t+π/3). Using the good-dielectric approximation, the source finds σ ≈ 4.17×10⁻³ S/m, α ≈ 0.497 Np/m, a half-amplitude distance of ln(2)/α ≈ 1.394 m, β ≈ 99.34 rad/m, λ ≈ 6.3 cm, v ≈ 1.897×10⁸ m/s and η ≈ 238.43∠0.286° Ω. The amplitude of H is approximately 0.210 A/m, its direction is +z; its phase lags that of E by the impedance angle.

For the example Ẽ = ây 10 exp[−j(6x+8z)], k = 10 rad/m and the propagation unit vector is 0.6âx + 0.8âz. The solution assumes ω = 10⁹ and uses v = 10⁸ m/s, εr = 9, η = 40π Ω. H̃ = (−0.8âx + 0.6âz) exp[−j(6x+8z)]/(4π).

## Homework 5: Polarization and Lossy Media
Examples for propagation in the −z direction:

- E = âx cos(ωt+βz) + ây sin(ωt+βz): circular.
- E = âx cos(ωt+βz) − ây sin(ωt+βz): circular, with the opposite sense of rotation.
- E = âx cos(ωt+βz) − 2ây sin(ωt+βz−45°): elliptical.

In a medium with εr = 36, μr = 4, σ = 1 S/m, E = âz 100 exp(−αx) cos(10π×10⁸t−βx). Since σ/(ωε) = 1, the general lossy-medium relations are used. The solution gives α ≈ 57.18 Np/m, β ≈ 138.06 rad/m, η ≈ 105.67∠π/8 Ω. The direction of H is −y and its amplitude is approximately 0.946 exp(−αx); the average Poynting vector is approximately âx 43.7 exp(−2αx) W/m². At x=0, the power passing through 1 m² is 43.7 W.

## Homework 6: Oblique Incidence on a Perfect Conductor
A perpendicularly polarized wave with an amplitude of 120π V/m is incident from free space on a perfectly conducting surface at z=0 at an angle of 37°. ω = 3×10⁹ rad/s. The source uses sin37° ≈ 0.6 and cos37° ≈ 0.8.

- Ẽᵢ = ây 120π exp[−j(6x+8z)].
- H̃ᵢ = (−0.8âx+0.6âz) exp[−j(6x+8z)].
- Ẽᵣ = −ây 120π exp[−j(6x−8z)].
- H̃ᵣ = (−0.8âx−0.6âz) exp[−j(6x−8z)].
- Total Ẽ = −jây 240π exp(−j6x) sin8z.
- Total H̃ = −1.6âx exp(−j6x)cos8z − j1.2âz exp(−j6x)sin8z.
- The average power flux is âx 144π sin²8z W/m²; the net average power normal to the surface is zero.

## Homework 7: Total Reflection and Dielectric Boundary
A 100 MHz wave is incident from water (εr = 80, σ = 0) on air at an angle of 60°. For perpendicular polarization, the critical angle is 6.42°; total reflection occurs. The source gives Γ = 1∠115.3° and, for the attenuated field in air, α₂ = 16.087 Np/m ≈ 139.7 dB/m.

In the second problem, the amplitude of the electric field normally incident from free space on a medium with εr = 4, μr = 1 is 120π V/m. Γ = −1/3, τ = 2/3, k₂ = 2k₀. The reflected E amplitude is −40π and the transmitted E amplitude is 80π; the H amplitudes are 1/3 and 4/3 A/m. The average powers are 60π for the incident wave, 20π/3 for the reflected wave and 160π/3 W/m² for the transmitted wave. The reflected power ratio is 11.11%.
