EEM214 Electronics I: Course Content Form (2013–2014 Spring)

EEM214 – Elektronik I · 14.02.2014 · 16.10.2026

This post is the course content form (syllabus) for EEM214 Electronics I in the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Başkent University, 2013–2014 spring semester. The course instructor is Assist. Prof. Dr. Derya Yılmaz, and the form is dated 14 February 2014. The other EEM214 posts in the archive consist of the course's presentations, assignments, and exams. The original Word file of the form can be downloaded at the end of the post.

Weekly topics

WeekTopics
1Atomic structure, semiconductor materials, P-type and N-type materials
2Diodes, diode applications
3Basic rectifier circuits
4Zener diode, other types of diodes
5Structure and characteristics of BJTs
6CE, CB, CC amplifier circuits and biasing
7Small-signal model of BJTs, gain, input and output impedances
8Midterm exam
9Types, structures, and characteristics of FETs
10FET amplifier circuits and biasing
11Small-signal model of FETs, gain, input and output impedances
12Multistage amplifiers, cascade and cascode connections
13Amplifier circuits with a microelectronic approach, current source circuits, and biasing transistors with current sources
14Internal structure of operational amplifiers
