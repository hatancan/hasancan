This post is the course content form (syllabus) for **EEM214 Electronics I** in the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Başkent University, 2013–2014 spring semester. The course instructor is Assist. Prof. Dr. Derya Yılmaz, and the form is dated 14 February 2014. The other EEM214 posts in the archive consist of the course's presentations, assignments, and exams. The original Word file of the form can be downloaded at the end of the post.

## Course resources

- *Microelectronic Circuits*, 6th Edition, Adel S. Sedra and Kenneth C. Smith, Oxford University Press
- *Microelectronic Circuit Design*, 4/e, Richard Jaeger and Travis Blalock, McGraw-Hill

### Additional resources

- *Electronic Devices and Circuit Theory*, 10th Edition, Robert L. Boylestad and Louis Nashelsky, Pearson
- *Microelectronics Circuit Analysis and Design*, 4th Edition, Donald M. Neamen, McGraw-Hill
- *KC's Problems and Solutions for Microelectronic Circuits*, Fourth Edition, Kenneth C. Smith, Oxford University Press
- *Laboratory Explorations for Microelectronic Circuits*, Kenneth C. Smith, Oxford University Press

## Weekly topics

| Week | Topics |
|---|---|
| 1 | Atomic structure, semiconductor materials, P-type and N-type materials |
| 2 | Diodes, diode applications |
| 3 | Basic rectifier circuits |
| 4 | Zener diode, other types of diodes |
| 5 | Structure and characteristics of BJTs |
| 6 | CE, CB, CC amplifier circuits and biasing |
| 7 | Small-signal model of BJTs, gain, input and output impedances |
| 8 | Midterm exam |
| 9 | Types, structures, and characteristics of FETs |
| 10 | FET amplifier circuits and biasing |
| 11 | Small-signal model of FETs, gain, input and output impedances |
| 12 | Multistage amplifiers, cascade and cascode connections |
| 13 | Amplifier circuits with a microelectronic approach, current source circuits, and biasing transistors with current sources |
| 14 | Internal structure of operational amplifiers |
