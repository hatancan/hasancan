EEM202 Circuit Theory II: 2011–2012 Spring Laboratory Schedule

EEM202 – Devre Teorisi II · 14.02.2012 · 18.10.2026

Laboratory information booklet for the EEM202 Circuit Theory II course at Başkent University, spring semester 2011–2012. It contains an experiment schedule and grading distribution that differ from the 2014–2015 booklet in the archive. The semester schedule begins on 14 February 2012; the archive dates were given relative to this start. The four-page PDF can be downloaded at the end of the post; the student fields on the last page are a blank template.

Grading

ComponentWeight
Pre-lab work25%
Experimental work20%
Report30%
Performance and oral exam25%

The weights add up to 100%. The booklet requires a minimum of 50% of the laboratory total and stipulates that no two or more experiments may be missed without an excuse. These are the archived rules of the 2012 semester.

Laboratory procedure

Pre-lab assignments are distributed one week before the experiment and are submitted on the laboratory day. A student who has not completed the pre-lab work cannot perform the experiment. Except in cases of necessity, each student is expected to perform the experiment themselves.

At the end of the session, the circuit is shown to the teaching assistant, who may ask questions or request changes. The components are returned and the bench is tidied up. Reports are submitted at the end of the relevant laboratory session. A 15% deduction is specified for one day of delay, and a grade of zero for two or more days.

Report format

Reports and pre-lab assignments, which are to be prepared in Turkish, must use the blank cover page template, be written on white unlined A4 paper, and show the page number and total number of pages. Items must be written clearly, and the pages must be stapled. It is also stated that leaving the laboratory without tidying up results in a 10% deduction from the experiment grade.

Note: In the cover page template on the last page, the year is still 2011 and the day options remain Thursday/Friday. The semester heading is 2011–2012 spring, while the laboratory days in the schedule are Tuesday/Friday. This template inconsistency has been preserved in the source.
