## Learning outcomes

1. Gain problem-solving ability.
2. Determine the field vectors of charge and current distributions in electrostatic and magnetostatic problems.
3. Know and use the laws underlying Maxwell's equations in differential and integral form.
4. Calculate the stored electric and magnetic energies in given geometries.
5. Calculate capacitance and inductance values.
6. Interpret natural phenomena using theoretical knowledge.

There are no prerequisites; mastery of first-year mathematics and physics courses is expected. No other recommended courses are specified. Lectures, question–answer sessions, and problem-solving methods are used; internship/practical work is not considered appropriate.
