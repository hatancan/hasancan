## Questions

1. Assume that the phase-shift method is used for a USSB signal with carrier f_c = f₁ + f₂, where the lower cutoff frequency of the first BPF is f₁ and that of the second BPF is f₂. x(t) denotes a voice signal with negligible frequency components outside 200 Hz < |f| < 3200 Hz. If the BPF has a transition region of 2β ≥ 0.01 f_LO, calculate the maximum value of f_c. Make the calculation on the spectrum, drawing the necessary graphs.
2. When the amplitude modulators in the figure are unbalanced, one nonlinear element is V_out = a₁V_in + a₂V_in² + a₃V_in³ while the other is V_out = b₁V_in + b₂V_in² + b₃V_in³. Calculate the output signal in this case. (Figures: balanced modulator — two AM modulators with inputs +½x(t) and −½x(t), carrier A_c cos ω_c t, outputs subtracted in a summer; AM modulator — ½x(t) is added to A_c cos ω_c t and passed through the nonlinear element and the BPF.)

## Solution 1

System: x(t) → mixer (f₁) → BPF₁ → mixer (f₂) → BPF₂ → s(t), f_c = f₁ + f₂. The voice spectrum lies between f_ℓ = 200 Hz and f_m = 3200 Hz.

BPF₁: since the gap between the two sidebands is 2f_ℓ = 400 Hz, the transition region is 2β = 400 Hz. Since the condition is 2β ≥ 0.01 f₁, we have 400 ≥ 0.01 f₁ and f₁ ≤ 40 kHz.

BPF₂: at the output of the first mixer, the gap between the sidebands is 2f₁ + 400 Hz; hence 2f₁ + 400 ≥ 0.01 f₂. For the maximum f₁ = 40 kHz, f₂ ≤ 100(2f₁ + 400) = 100 × 80.4 kHz = 8.04 MHz.

Result: f_c = f₁ + f₂ < 8.08 MHz (f_c,max ≈ 8.08 MHz).

## Solution 2

V_out+ = a₁(A_c cos ω_c t + ½x) + a₂(A_c cos ω_c t + ½x)² + a₃(A_c cos ω_c t + ½x)³
V_out− = b₁(A_c cos ω_c t − ½x) + b₂(A_c cos ω_c t − ½x)² + b₃(A_c cos ω_c t − ½x)³

Expansions: cos²ω_c t = ½ + ½cos 2ω_c t; cos³ω_c t = ¾ cos ω_c t + ¼ cos 3ω_c t. The BPFs reject everything outside f_c − W < |f| < f_c + W; x_c(t) = V_out+|BPF − V_out−|BPF. The result on the paper:

x_c(t) = (a₁ + ¾a₃ − b₁ − ¾b₃) cos ω_c t + 2(a₂ + b₂) x(t) cos ω_c t + 3(a₃ − b₃) x²(t) cos ω_c t

Result: there is an unsuppressed carrier and distortion (due to the second-order term); that is, if the two nonlinear elements are not identical, the balanced modulator cannot fully suppress the carrier.

*Note: The coefficients on the paper do not include the A_c factors. In our own expansion with the ½x input, the coefficient of the cross term is (a₂ + b₂)A_c, the coefficient of the x² term is ¾(a₃ − b₃)A_c, and the coefficient of the carrier term is (a₁ + ¾a₃A_c² − b₁ − ¾b₃A_c²). The nature of the result (an unsuppressed carrier and distortion) does not change.*
