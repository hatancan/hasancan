## Group A
Parallel plates at y=e and y=e². σ=2y S/m, ε=4ε₀y F/m, V₀=1 V, S=1 m². Fringing is neglected. From ∇·J=0 we get J=Kây; from V₀=∫K/(2y)dy=K/2, K=2.

- J=2ây A/m², E=ây/y, D=4ε₀ây.
- P=ε₀(4−1/y)ây; ρpv=−ε₀/y².
- Polarization charge on the lower surface is ε₀(1/e−4); on the upper surface it is ε₀(4−1/e²).
- Volume charge is ε₀(1/e²−1/e); the surface charges sum to the opposite of this, so the net is zero.
- I=2 A, R=1/2 Ω; free charge density on the lower plate is 4ε₀.
- C=4ε₀ F, energy 2ε₀ J.
- Pressure at y=e² is 2ε₀/e² N/m², in the +y direction.

## Group B
Plates at y=0 and y=ln2. σ=4e⁻ʸ, ε=2ε₀e⁻ʸ; V₀=1 V and S=1 m². J=Kây; since V₀=(K/4)(2−1), K=4.

- J=4ây, E=eʸây, D=2ε₀ây.
- P=ε₀(2−eʸ)ây; ρpv=ε₀eʸ.
- Polarization surface charge at y=0 is −ε₀, zero at the top; volume charge is ε₀ and the net is zero.
- I=4 A, R=1/4 Ω; free charge density on the upper plate is −2ε₀.
- C=2ε₀, energy ε₀ J.
- Pressure on the lower dielectric surface is ε₀ N/m², in the −y direction.
