## Research topics

**Digital signal processing:** Adaptive-resolution time-frequency analyses; speech processing and determination of speaker characteristics; applications in music theory.

**Statistics:** Development of test statistics; random number generators, Q-RNG, P-RNG, and T-RNG.

**Antenna design and optimization:** Antenna arrays; genetic, PSO, and fuzzy methods.

**Music theory:** The natural-37 interval tone system and makams; Al-Jurjani's 1000-year-old problem; the fifth and octave conflict.

## Graduate thesis proposals

The announcement proposes master's and doctoral theses in the areas of digital signal processing, statistics, antenna design and optimization, electromagnetic propagation, and music theory. Examples:

1. Investigation of sound characteristics using adaptive time-frequency analyses.
2. Development of antenna design and effective optimization methods.
3. Investigation of the statistical performance of different random number generators.
4. Investigation of electromagnetic propagation prediction methods.
