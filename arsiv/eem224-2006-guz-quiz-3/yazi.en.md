## Group A: normal incidence
One quarter of the power of a unit-amplitude, z-polarized wave incident from a dielectric onto air is reflected. The permeability of the medium is μ₀. The solution finds εr = 9, η₁ = η₀/3, k₁ = 3k₀, Γ = 1/2, and τ = 3/2.

- Eᵢ = âz exp(−j3k₀y), Hᵢ = âx (3/η₀) exp(−j3k₀y).
- Eᵣ = âz (1/2) exp(j3k₀y), Hᵣ = −âx [3/(2η₀)] exp(j3k₀y).
- Eₜ = âz (3/2) exp(−jk₀y), Hₜ = âx [3/(2η₀)] exp(−jk₀y).
- Average power densities: incident ây 3/(2η₀), reflected −ây 3/(8η₀), transmitted ây 9/(8η₀).

Polarization examples: [3√2 ây + (3−3j) exp(−jπ/4) âz] exp(jkx) is circular; (2ây + j5âx) exp(jkz) is elliptical.

## Group B: magnetic medium
A unit-amplitude, y-polarized wave incident from air onto a medium with ε = ε₀, μ = μrμ₀ has 1/9 of its power reflected. The solution uses the results μr = 4, η₂ = 2η₀, k₂ = 2k₀, Γ = 1/3, τ = 4/3. The incident and transmitted waves travel in the +z direction; the reflected wave travels in the −z direction. The electric field amplitudes are 1, 1/3, and 4/3; the magnetic field amplitudes are 1/η₀, 1/(3η₀), and 2/(3η₀), respectively. The average powers are 1/(2η₀), 1/(18η₀), 4/(9η₀).

[√8 âz + (2+2j) exp(jπ/4) âx] exp(−jky) is circular; (2âx − j3ây) exp(jkz) is elliptical polarization. These are the polarization examples.

*Note: The reflected power ratio gives only |Γ|². The positive Γ branch chosen by the source solution was transcribed; the power ratio alone cannot rule out the other possible impedance branch. The handedness of the polarization was not named, since the viewing direction is not specified.*
