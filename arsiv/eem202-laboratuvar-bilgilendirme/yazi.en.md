This post is the **Laboratory Information Booklet** prepared for the laboratory section of the Başkent University Electrical-Electronics Engineering course **EEM202 Circuit Theory II** (2014-2015 Spring Semester, March 2015). The laboratory was run by Research Assistants Tunç Murat İlgar and Gencer Tulay. The fourth page of the booklet was a sample cover page filled in by the student (it has been removed because it contained a name and student number); the PDF of the first three pages can be downloaded at the end of the post.

## How the laboratory works

1. The laboratory preliminary work is obtained from the relevant web page one week before the laboratory day, and students submit their preliminary work on the laboratory day. Students who come to the laboratory without having completed their preliminary work cannot do the laboratory work.
1. Each student will perform the experiments on their own. Group work will not be permitted except in cases of necessity.
1. The student is responsible for the proper functioning of the materials or experiment set to be used. The student is responsible for any situation that could affect the experimental result during the experimental work (such as a short circuit on the breadboard).
1. At the end of the experimental work, students must show their completed work to the assistant on duty. The assistant on duty records each student's completed work. When necessary, the assistant may ask the student questions about the work done or ask them to make changes to it.
1. At the end of the experimental work, each student hands in their preliminary work and the documents and materials related to the experimental work to the assistant on duty. They then clear away the laboratory equipment and the experiment bench.
1. Experiment reports are submitted at the end of the relevant laboratory hour. A penalty is applied to that week's experiment grade for late reports: −15% for 1 day of delay; for 2 or more days, the laboratory grade is taken as zero.

> **Important:** There is no make-up opportunity for experiments missed without an official excuse, and the grade for that experiment will be counted as 0. Students who miss 2 or more experiments without an official excuse will fail the course. Students who do not receive at least 50% of the full laboratory grade will be considered to have received an F for the entire course.

## General rules

1. Walking around inside the laboratory and leaving without permission during the experiment is prohibited.
1. Eating and drinking anything inside the laboratory is prohibited.
1. Each student is responsible for the experiment set and materials they use in their work.
1. Students who do not return the laboratory materials at the end of the experiment, or who leave without clearing the experiment bench, will have their laboratory grade for that week reduced by 10%.

## Preliminary work and report format

- The attached cover page must be included in the preliminary work and reports to be prepared. Preliminary work and reports without a cover page will not be evaluated.
- Except for work uploaded to the web page, all preliminary work and experiment reports are submitted written on plain white A4 paper. Reports or preliminary work written on notebook pages, scrap paper, or lined/squared paper will absolutely not be accepted.
- What is requested in the preliminary work and report will be written clearly in itemized form.
- Preliminary work and reports will be prepared in Turkish.
- The total number of pages and the page number must be indicated on every page.
- Preliminary work and reports to be submitted will be stapled at the top left corner. Please do not submit your work inside a plastic sleeve. If preliminary work and reports that are not stapled are lost, the responsibility belongs to the student.

> **Warning:** Students who cheat or let others cheat in any way will have their grade for the relevant experiment taken as zero, and the relevant disciplinary regulation will be applied.
