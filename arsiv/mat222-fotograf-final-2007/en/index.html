MAT222 Differential Equations: January 11, 2007 Final Exam Questions (Institution Not Verified)

MAT222 – Diferansiyel Denklemler · 11.01.2007 · 17.10.2026

This post was transcribed to text from photographed pages of a "Differential Equations" final exam paper dated January 11, 2007. The header reads "Faculty of Engineering, Department of Electrical Engineering"; the university name is outside the frame. The instructor's full name is also not visible. The student's name and number were not transcribed. The photographs are kept in a private archive; this post contains no photographs or downloadable files.

This content belongs to a third-party source; it is shared for archival and nostalgic purposes with the source credited. It will be removed at the request of the rights holder.

Differential equations

  1. Show that the function y = C₁ cos(ωx) + C₂ sin(ωx) is a solution of the equation y″ + ω²y = 0.
  2. Find the general solution of the equation xy dx + (1 + x)(1 − x) dy = 0.
  3. Find the general solution of the equation y⁽⁵⁾ − y⁽⁴⁾ + 2y‴ − 2y″ + y′ − y = 0.
  4. Determine whether the functions eˣ, −sin x, and cos x are linearly dependent.
  5. Find the general solution of the equation (2x/y³) dx + [(y² − 3x²)/y⁴] dy = 0.
  6. Find the general solution of the equation xy″ + y′ = x².

Integrating factor question

On the seventh line, only "Find the integrating factor in the form µ = µ(x² + y²)" can be read. The differential equation that this instruction refers to is not visible in the photographs.

Note: It is unclear which equation the seventh question applies to; it was not linked to the previous question and the missing equation was not guessed.

Laplace transform questions

Question eight: Find the Laplace transform of the function f(t) = e^((α + 1)t) sin(βt).

Question nine: Using the Laplace transform, find the solution satisfying y″ − 5y′ + 6y = 1, y(0) = 1, y′(0) = 0.

Visible part of the last question

In question ten, equations of the form (ax + b)ⁿy⁽ⁿ⁾ + p₁(ax + b)ⁿ⁻¹y⁽ⁿ⁻¹⁾ + p₂(ax + b)ⁿ⁻²y⁽ⁿ⁻²⁾ + … and the phrase "solution of the equations" can be read. Because the right edge is cut off, the final terms and the full instruction were not transcribed.

Transcription check

The photographs contain no solution key. The following calculations were done to check the expressions as read:

  • In question one, y″ = −ω²y.
  • The characteristic polynomial of question three factors as (r − 1)(r² + 1)².
  • The Wronskian of the functions in question four is 2eˣ; since it is nonzero, they are linearly independent.
  • The left-hand side of question five is d(x²/y³ − 1/y), with y ≠ 0.
  • In question six, (xy′)′ = x²; on intervals where x ≠ 0, y = x³/9 + C₁ ln|x| + C₂.
  • The transform in question eight is β/[(s − α − 1)² + β²], with s > α + 1 as a sufficient convergence condition.
  • In question nine, y = 1/6 + (5/2)e²ˣ − (5/3)e³ˣ satisfies both the equation and the initial conditions.

These results are not solutions written in the source; they are checks performed during the text transcription.
