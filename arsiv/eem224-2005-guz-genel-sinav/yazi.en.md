## Question 1: Plane wave

In a medium with μ=μ₀, H(x,t)=0.05 cos(10⁹t−5x)â_y A/m is given.

Results: propagation in +x; v=2×10⁸ m/s; ε_r=9/4; η=80π Ω; λ=0.4π m.

Ẽ=−4πe^(−j5x)â_z V/m,

E(x,t)=−4π cos(10⁹t−5x)â_z V/m.

S_av=0.1πâ_x W/m².

## Question 2: Rotating square loop

Side a=4 mm, angular velocity ω₁=6π×10⁶ rad/s, B=5×10⁻³sin(6π×10⁶t)â_z Wb/m². At t=0 the loop is parallel to the x-y plane.

Φ=BS cos(ω₁t)=40×10⁻⁹sin(2ω₁t).

Depending on the polarity of the source, V_ind=−0.48π cos(12π×10⁶t) V.

## Question 3: Oblique incidence from a dielectric into air

In the region z≤0, ε_r=3, μ_r=1; air lies on the z>0 side.

E_inc=(4â_x+4√3â_y−4â_z)cos(ωt−2π√3y−6πz) V/m.

θ_i=30°, k=4π√3 rad/m, f=600 MHz, λ=1/(2√3) m. Snell's law gives θ_t=60°; the parallel component is not reflected at the Brewster angle.

For the perpendicular component, Γ_⊥=1/2; Ẽ_r=2â_x exp[−j(2π√3y−6πz)]. The total transmitted field is:

E_t=(6â_x+4√3â_y−12â_z)cos(ωt−2π√3y−2πz) V/m,

ω=12π×10⁸ rad/s.

## Question 4: Finding the load from a standing wave

On a lossless line with Z₀=100 Ω, V_max=1.4 mV, V_min=0.2 mV, the spacing between consecutive minima is 40 cm, and the distance from the load to the first minimum is 30 cm.

S=7, |Γ_L|=(S−1)/(S+1)=0.75, λ=0.8 m, β=2.5π rad/m. From the minimum position, Γ_L=j0.75 is found.

Z_L=Z₀(1+Γ_L)/(1−Γ_L)=28+j96 Ω.

In the part on building the same input impedance with a 100 Ω line and a resistor R_m>100 Ω, the result is given as R_m=700 Ω, L_m=0.3 m.
