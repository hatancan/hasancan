SOS204 Business Administration: Personal SWOT Analysis Assignment

SOS204 – İşletme · 18.10.2017 · 15.11.2026

A personal SWOT analysis assignment prepared for the SOS204 Business Administration course: the author lists their strengths and weaknesses, along with the opportunities and threats arising from the internal and external environment, one by one. The text of the assignment is below; the PDF with the cover page redacted can be downloaded at the end of the post.

Note: Personal names in this post and on the cover in the download have been changed or redacted; the author of the assignment is referred to here as "Kerem Aksoy". The date on the cover is Word's default value (30 December 1899) and is therefore meaningless; the post date is the date the file was saved.

Purpose of the Assignment

In the assignment, a personal SWOT analysis was carried out by identifying strengths, weaknesses, and the opportunities and threats arising from the internal and external environment.

Strengths

  • Quality management training and a certificate were obtained from TÜRKAK. It is possible to contribute to the quality management of the company one works for, or to work in compliance with quality management systems.
  • Sales engineering training and a certificate were obtained. As an engineer at a company, the product being worked on can be developed while also partly evaluating it from the perspective of the customer and the sales specialist.
  • One year was spent at the Ekin Pre-Incubation Center, and entrepreneurship courses were taken. During that year, general knowledge of entrepreneurship was gained and an innovative perspective was developed. In addition, many entrepreneurship competitions were entered at various institutions, and there was the opportunity to meet different entrepreneurs and mentors.
  • Different project management trainings were taken from different authorities, and an effort was made to learn project management from start to finish. It is known how to behave and what to do in the projects one will be assigned to.
  • Care was taken to understand the practical counterparts of the theoretical courses in the undergraduate program; when sitting across from a colleague from the world of electronics and software, one can hold a conversation knowing what is being said.
  • Computer languages and prototype development methods that are not part of university curricula were learned through one's own efforts.
  • Work has been carried out for about 10 years at the advertising company owned by the family, with care taken to take part in every department.
  • Graphics and design programs can be used.
  • Since business administration is being studied at the Open Education Faculty, there is knowledge of business administration.
  • Since books, magazines, websites and television programs about physics have been followed for years, current developments around the world in the main field, electronics, are also followed.
  • Since a daily newspaper is read, the world is followed.
  • Since mathematics is known well, one can think analytically.

Weaknesses

  • Taking on far too much work has become a habit; even when the work undertaken is completed, poor work results because of this.
  • Despite being within the university's system, mistakes were made through not taking it seriously; a low grade point average appears as a minus point when one wants to achieve goals.
  • English cannot be spoken.
  • Being very hasty causes work to be redone frequently and a great deal of time to be lost.
  • One becomes demoralized quickly; constant self-motivation is required.

Opportunities

  • The undergraduate program will be completed in 3 months; there will be time for the projects one wants to carry out.
  • The Electromagnetics course currently being taken is a good opportunity to gain an advantage in the application for the physics graduate program one wants to pursue; one must be very successful in this course.
  • The software company where the opportunity to work arose during the undergraduate program will allow a clear decision to be made, while the degree is still ongoing, on whether to continue in this field.
  • Thanks to the advantage provided by the family's company and the software knowledge, various e-commerce trials can be carried out at no cost. This could be a good business that can be managed remotely.

Threats

  • Because so much work has been taken on, it may not be possible to carry all of it.
  • Since all money is spent on education and projects, it may not be possible to come up with money.
  • The projects under development may not be meaningful tomorrow; it is necessary to act quickly.

Note: The assignment was written in the first person; here the narration has been changed to the third person, but the content is the same.
