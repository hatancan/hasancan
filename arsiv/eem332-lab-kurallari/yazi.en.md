### Laboratory Procedures

1- Laboratory preliminary work is obtained from the relevant web page one week before the laboratory day, and students submit their preliminary work at the laboratory session. Students who come to the laboratory without having completed their preliminary work cannot perform the laboratory work, and no make-up opportunity is granted for the relevant experiment.

2- Each student will perform the experiments individually. Group work will not be permitted except in cases of necessity.

3- The student is responsible for the proper operation of the materials or experiment set to be used. Accordingly, students must report any faults in these materials or experiment sets to the research assistant in charge of the laboratory before starting the experiment.

4- During the experimental work, students must show the work they have completed to the assistant on duty in the laboratory.        The assistant on duty records the work completed by each student. When necessary, the assistant may ask the student questions about the work done or may ask the student to make changes to it.

5- A quiz may be given during the laboratory session without prior notice. If a quiz is given, the quiz grades account for 50% of the Performance and Oral grade.

6- At the end of the experimental work, each student shows the documents related to the experimental work to the assistant on duty. Students are obliged to leave the laboratory equipment and the experiment bench in an orderly condition at the end of the experiment.

7- Experiment reports must comply with the given format and be submitted within one week following the laboratory session. For reports submitted late, the penalty given below is applied to the entire grade of that week's experiment (Preliminary Work + Experimental Work + Report + Performance and Oral).

1 day late -%15 2 days late -%30 3 days late -%50 For 4 days or more, the laboratory grade of the relevant experiment is evaluated as zero.

8- Provided that they have an official excuse, students are granted 1 make-up opportunity during the semester. The laboratory staff must be contacted regarding the time of the make-up experiment.

### General Rules

1- Walking around the laboratory and leaving without permission during the experiment are prohibited.

2- Eating and drinking anything in the laboratory is prohibited.

3- Each student is responsible for the computer, experiment set, and materials they use.

4- The laboratory grades of students who do not return the laboratory materials at the end of the experiment, or who leave without tidying the experiment/computer desk, are reduced by 5 points.

5- All preliminary work and experiment reports are to be submitted in the given report format, written on plain (unlined) white A4 paper. Reports or preliminary work written on notebook pages, scratch paper, or lined/squared paper will not be accepted under any circumstances.

6- The relevant experiment grades of students who cheat or allow others to copy, in any way whatsoever, are evaluated as zero.

### Report Format

Cover: The preliminary work and reports to be prepared must each have a cover page. The page given in the Appendix will be used as the cover page. Work without a cover page will not be accepted.

Preliminary Work and Experimental Work: The information requested in these sections will be written clearly in list form. Bringing a copy of the code/programs you write in your preliminary work to the computer

```asm
ortamında bir kopyasının getirilmesi, laboratuvar sırasında sizlere kolaylık sağlayabilir.
```

It is recommended that the total number of pages and the page number be indicated on each page. The preliminary work and reports to be submitted will be stapled with a metal staple. Please do not submit your work in a plastic sleeve.

## eem332labkurallari

Başkent University Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering EEM 332 – Microprocessors 2010-2011 Spring Semester Laboratory Information Sheet

### A) LABORATORY PROCEDURES

1- The laboratory procedures are explained in this document.

2- Each experiment consists of preliminary work and experimental work performed during the laboratory sessions.

3- The distribution of the grades you will receive from each experiment and the related explanations are given in the GRADING section.

4- The work schedule to be followed throughout the semester is given in the EXPERIMENT SCHEDULE section. Changes may be made to this schedule in line with the requests of the course instructor or according to changes made by the department head's office. These changes are announced on the relevant web page.

5- Laboratory preliminary work is obtained from the relevant web page one week before the laboratory day. The web addresses are given in the CONTACT INFORMATION section. Students submit their preliminary work on the laboratory day within the first 10 minutes of the experiment.

Students who come to the laboratory without having completed their preliminary work cannot perform the laboratory work. The entire grade of the relevant experiment is evaluated as 0 (zero) for students who are later found not to have submitted their preliminary work during the experiment.

Experiments that do not include preliminary work will be announced by the laboratory staff.

6- Once preliminary work has been submitted in written form, it will under no circumstances be returned. It is the students' own responsibility to keep a copy of their preliminary work for use during the experiment.

7- Each student will perform the experiments individually. Group work will not be permitted except in cases of necessity.

8- At the beginning of the experiment, students must check that the equipment they use is in working order. They must report faulty equipment to the research assistant in charge of the laboratory before starting the experiment.

9- A quiz may be given during the experiment session without prior notice. If a quiz is given, the quiz grades constitute 50% of the Performance and Oral grade.

10- During and/or at the end of the experimental work, students must show the work they have completed to the assistant on duty in the laboratory. The responsible research assistant records the work completed by each student. When deemed necessary, the student may be asked questions about the work done or may be asked to make changes to it.

11- At the end of the experimental work, each student submits any documents and materials related to the experimental work to the assistant on duty. Students are obliged to leave the laboratory equipment and experiment benches in an orderly condition.

12- Provided that they have an official excuse approved by the department, students are granted make-up opportunities during the semester. The laboratory staff must be contacted regarding the time of the make-up experiment.

13- 80% attendance in the laboratories is mandatory. Students who exceed the stated absence rate without an official excuse are considered to have failed the course.

### B) GENERAL RULES

1- Students must be ready at the place of the experiment at the scheduled time. Students who are more than 10 minutes late to the experiment are not admitted to that session. If there is a second session, they may come at the start of it and perform their experiments. No additional time is given to these students to complete the experiment.

If the reason for the delay is a problem with the shuttle services, students may join the experiment after 10 minutes with a written statement obtained from the relevant unit.

2- Walking around the laboratory and leaving without permission during the experiment are prohibited.

3- Eating and drinking anything in the laboratory is prohibited.

4- Each student is responsible for the experiment set and materials they use.

5- The relevant experiment grades of students who cheat or allow others to copy, in any way whatsoever, are evaluated as zero.

### C) GRADING

1- The table given below is used for grading each experiment.

Preliminary Work         Experimental Work     Performance and Oral %20                  %30                    %50

2- If it is not possible to apply all of the grading items given in the table for an experiment, the grade obtained from the applicable items is converted to a 100-point scale. (E.g., the grade for an experiment without preliminary work will be out of 70. If a student receives 35/70 on this experiment, the grade will be converted to 50/100.)
