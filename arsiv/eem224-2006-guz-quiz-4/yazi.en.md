## Group A
In air, Eᵢ=(2.4âx+1.8ây+3âz)cos(ωt−6πx+8πy) is incident on a conductor at x>0. k=10π, f=1.5 GHz, λ=0.2 m; the direction of incidence is 0.6âx−0.8ây, and the angle with the normal is approximately 53°.

Ẽᵢ=(2.4âx+1.8ây+3âz)exp[−j(6πx−8πy)]. H̃ᵢ=(−2.4âx−1.8ây+3âz)exp[−j(6πx−8πy)]/(120π).

Reflected Ẽᵣ=(2.4âx−1.8ây−3âz)exp[j(6πx+8πy)], H̃ᵣ=(2.4âx−1.8ây+3âz)exp[j(6πx+8πy)]/(120π). The average powers are Pᵢ=(5.4âx−7.2ây)/(120π), Pᵣ=(−5.4âx−7.2ây)/(120π) W/m².

## Group B
Eᵢ=(5âx+6ây+8âz)cos(ωt−8πy+6πz) is incident on a conductor at y>0. f=1.5 GHz, λ=0.2 m; the direction of incidence is 0.8ây−0.6âz, and the angle is approximately 37°.

Ẽᵢ=(5âx+6ây+8âz)exp[−j(8πy−6πz)]. H̃ᵢ=(10âx−3ây−4âz)exp[−j(8πy−6πz)]/(120π).

Ẽᵣ=(−5âx+6ây−8âz)exp[j(8πy+6πz)], H̃ᵣ=(10âx+3ây−4âz)exp[j(8πy+6πz)]/(120π). The average powers are Pᵢ=(100ây−75âz)/(240π), Pᵣ=(−100ây−75âz)/(240π) W/m². The normal power component is reflected back, while the tangential component retains its direction.
