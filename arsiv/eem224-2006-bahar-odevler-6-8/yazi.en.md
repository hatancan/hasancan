## Homework 6: Variable Dielectric
The plates are at y=1/2 and y=1, with ε=ε₀/y; V₀=3 V, area 1 m². D, E, and P are asked for; also the polarization volume and surface charges; the free charge on the lower plate; the capacitance, energy, and the pressure and force at y=1/2. It is to be shown that the total polarization charge is zero. There is no separate solution page for this homework.

## Homework 7: Conducting Dielectric
In the same gap, σ=3/y, ε=6ε₀/y; V₀=1 V, area 3 m². J=8ây A/m², E=(8y/3)ây, D=16ε₀ây, P=ε₀(16−8y/3)ây.

ρpv=8ε₀/3; the lower surface charge is −44ε₀/3, and the upper surface 40ε₀/3. The total volume charge is 4ε₀ and the surface charge is −4ε₀. I=24 A, R=1/24 Ω. The free charge density on the lower plate is 16ε₀; C=48ε₀, and the energy is 24ε₀ J. The pressure on the lower dielectric surface is 32ε₀/3 N/m², in the −y direction.

## Homework 8: Coaxial Magnetic System
The inner conductor has radius a=0.5 m; between b=1 and c=2 m, μ=2μ₀r. The inner conductor carries I=2 A in the +z direction, and the outer thin shell carries the return current.

- 0<r<0.5: Jz=8/π, Hφ=4r/π, Bφ=4μ₀r/π.
- 0.5<r<1: Hφ=1/(πr), Bφ=μ₀/(πr).
- 1<r<2: Hφ=1/(πr), Bφ=2μ₀/π.
- r>2: H=B=0.

In the magnetic material, M=(2/π−1/(πr))âφ; Jm=2âz/(πr). At r=1, Jms=âz/π; at r=2, Jms=−3âz/(2π). The total volume current is 4 A, and the surface currents are 2 and −6 A; the net is zero.

The energy per unit length is μ₀/(4π)+(μ₀/π)ln2+2μ₀/π. The inductance is μ₀/(8π)+(μ₀/(2π))ln2+μ₀/π. At r=1, the pressure is μ₀/(2π²) N/m², in the −r direction.
