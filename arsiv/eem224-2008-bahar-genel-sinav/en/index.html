EEM224 Electromagnetics I: Spring 2008: Midterm Exam Questions

EEM224 – Elektromanyetik I · · 17.10.2026

This archive record, transcribed to text from photographed pages, contains the question texts along with the legible solution steps. The photographs are kept in a private archive; there is no downloadable file.

Personal information on a friend's exam paper was not transcribed. The source does not contain a solution.

Line charge, line current, and Lorentz force

A circular charge of radius b=3 m in the xy plane has a density of ρl=250ε₀/3 C/m. On the line y=−a=−2, z=c=4, there is a line current I=4/μ₀ A parallel to the x axis. Compute the B field of the current and the E field of the charge at the point (0,0,c). As a charge q=1 C passes through this point with velocity v₀=−4πâx m/s, find the total force.

Dielectric boundary

The medium y<0 is ε₁=2ε₀; the medium y>0 is ε₂=4ε₀. E₁=−3âx+4ây−2âz. D₁, P₁, E₂, D₂, P₂, and the bound polarization surface charge are requested. The boundary surface is the xz plane.

Coaxial magnetic structure

A very thin inner wire carries I in the z direction; the region 0<r<0.2 m is free space; the region 0.2<r<1 m is a magnetic material with μ=μ₀(1+r), σ=0. The outer conductor at r=1 m carries I in the opposite direction.

  1. Find H and B in each region, and plot |B(r)|.
  2. Find the equivalent magnetization sources M, Jm, and Jms.
  3. Show that the net total of the equivalent volume and surface currents is zero.
  4. Find the magnetic pressure at r=1 m and its direction.
  5. Calculate the energy per unit length and the inductance.

Partially dielectric-filled capacitor

The parallel plates between y=1 and y=2 m have an area of 1 m² and a voltage of 1 V. Half of the volume between the plates is filled with a dielectric ε(y)=4ε₀y, and the other half is free space. Field fringing is neglected. E, D, and P; the polarization volume and surface charges; the total polarization charge; the energy; the capacitance; and the pressure at the dielectric-air boundary are requested.
