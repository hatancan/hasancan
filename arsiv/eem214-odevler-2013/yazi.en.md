This post is the text of the assignments given in the spring 2013 semester of Başkent University's **EEM214 Electronics I** course. It consists of two Word documents: Assignment I (a survey of diode types) and Assignments 2–4 (introductory reports on a selected device based on its datasheet). All of them were assigned on March 12, 2013. The originals of both documents can be downloaded as a ZIP at the end of the post.

## Assignment I: Diode types

Assigned: 12.03.2013. Due: Wednesday, 20.03.2013, 17:00.

You are required to research tunnel diodes, varactor diodes, Schottky diodes, photodiodes, and LED diodes, and to prepare a report explaining the structure, symbol, operating principle, characteristic curve, and areas of use of each.

- Reports may be prepared on A4 paper of any kind (white, graph paper, squared paper, etc.), either by hand or on a computer, legibly and neatly. Do not make a cover page, and your student number must be written at the top left of the first page.
- Your assignment must be no more than 3 pages.
- The midterm exam will include a 5-point question on this assignment.
- Late assignments will absolutely not be accepted.

## Assignment 2: A diode

One diode will be selected from among diode types such as rectifier (ordinary diode), Zener, tunnel, varactor, Schottky, photodiode, etc. You will find the "data sheet" for this diode and prepare a report that introduces the device by explaining the properties of the diode you selected.

Assigned: 12.03.2013. Due: Monday, 25.03.2013, 17:00.

## Assignment 3: A BJT

A BJT-type transistor will be selected, and you will find the "data sheet" for this transistor and prepare a report that introduces the device by describing the properties of the transistor you selected.

Assigned: 12.03.2013. Due: Monday, 22.04.2013, 17:00.

## Assignment 4: A FET or MOSFET

A transistor will be selected from either of the FET (JFET) or MOSFET transistor types. You will find the "data sheet" for this transistor and prepare a report that introduces the device by explaining the properties of the transistor you selected.

Assigned: 12.03.2013. Due: Monday, 13.05.2013, 17:00.

## Rules to be followed for the assignments

1. The diode and transistor selections will be made by you, and each student's selection will be different. To ensure this, you will begin your work only after getting approval from the instructor for your selection: you will have the instructor write down the name of the device you selected; if another student has selected this device before you, you will have to choose a different device.
1. Some web addresses that may be useful for datasheets: alldatasheet.com, alldatasheets.com, alldatasheets.org, otxi.com, google.com, datasheetblog.com, measurementdb.com, electronicsharing.com. Besides these, you may also use device catalogs (available at technical service shops or at places that sell the device).
1. Reports will be prepared on A4 paper on a computer. Do not make a cover page, and your student number must be written at the top left of the first page.
1. The reports must not be a Turkish translation of the device's datasheet; that is, you will not translate it word for word. You will also draw inferences from the information given there.
1. A circuit containing the device you selected will be found (usually in the "application notes" section of the datasheet), and the role of the device in the circuit will be explained, showing the necessary calculations.
1. The datasheet of the device you selected will also be submitted together with the report.
1. Late assignments will absolutely not be accepted.
