## Type A: wave parameters from the magnetic field

In a medium with μ=μ₀, H(y,t)=0.06 cos(10⁹t−5y)â_z A/m is given. The propagation direction, velocity, ε_r, medium impedance, wavelength, the phasor of H, and the time expression of E are requested.

Propagation is in the +y direction; β=5 rad/m, v=ω/β=2×10⁸ m/s. ε_r=(c/v)²=9/4, η=η₀/√ε_r=80π Ω. λ=2π/5=0.4π m.

H̃=0.06e^(−j5y)â_z, E=−4.8π cos(10⁹t−5y)â_x V/m.

## Type A: rotating loop

A loop of radius 2 m rotates about the x axis at ω=2π×10⁶ rad/s. The field is constant at 10⁻⁶ Wb/m² over the interval 0<t<0.5×10⁻⁶ s. The solution gives Φ=4π×10⁻⁶ cos(ωt), V=8π² sin(ωt). The terminal sign depends on the polarity in the figure.

## Type B: wave parameters from the electric field

E(x,t)=377 cos(10¹⁰t−40x)â_z V/m, μ=μ₀.

Propagation is in the +x direction; v=2.5×10⁸ m/s, ε_r=1.44, η=100π Ω, λ=π/20=0.05π m. Ẽ=377e^(−j40x)â_z.

The source gives H≈−1.2 cos(10¹⁰t−40x)â_y A/m.

## Type B: rectangular loop next to a long wire

The sides of the rectangle are a=1 m, b=4 m; its distance to the long wire is d=4 m. The wire current increases linearly for a short time. The flux is found by integrating the wire's field B=μ₀I/(2πr) over the surface:

Φ=−μ₀Ia/(2π) ln[(d+b)/d]=−μ₀I/(2π)ln2.

During the interval in which the current increases linearly, the voltage is constant: V=μ₀ln2/(2π); at other times it is zero. The ideal discontinuities at the transition instants are left out of this piecewise solution.
