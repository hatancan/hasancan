## Quiz 2 B: line and point charges
A right semicircle of radius b=2 m, for −π/2≤φ≤π/2, carries ρl=ρ₀cosφ; ρ₀=−16πε₀. At the center, E=πâx V/m and V=−8 V. The work done by an external force to move a 10 C charge slowly from the center to infinity is 80 J.

In the second problem, a 4 μC charge is at (−4,2,−8) and a −12 μC charge is at (2,2,−6). E and V are asked at (−4,2,−6). The solution sets up Coulomb superposition with two source vectors; the potential contributions cancel each other.

## Test 3 B: conducting dielectric
For 0.5<y<1, σ=4/y and ε=2ε₀/y; V₀=3 V, S=1/4 m². Solution:

- J=32ây, E=8yây, D=16ε₀ây, P=8ε₀(2−y)ây.
- ρpv=8ε₀; the volume charge is ε₀.
- The polarization charge on the lower surface is −12ε₀ and on the upper surface 8ε₀; the total surface charge is −ε₀.
- I=8 A, R=3/8 Ω; the free charge density on the upper plate is −16ε₀.
- C=4ε₀/3, energy 6ε₀ J.
- At y=0.5 the pressure is 32ε₀ N/m², in the −y direction.

## Test 4 B: current field and boundary
The axes of a wire parallel to z and a cylinder are 7 m apart; the cylinder radius is 2 m. The wire carries 12π A in the +z direction, and the cylinder carries J=2âz A/m². The source question asks for the field at y=3, while the introductory sentence also states y=5. For y=3, the solution finds the cylinder contribution to be μ₀âx and the wire contribution to be −2μ₀âx; the total is −μ₀âx.

y<0: ε₁=8ε₀, σ₁=4; y>0: ε₂=6ε₀, σ₂=2. J₁=8ây+6âz. E₁=2ây+1.5âz, D₁=ε₀(16ây+12âz), P₁=ε₀(14ây+10.5âz). J₂=8ây+3âz, E₂=4ây+1.5âz, D₂=ε₀(24ây+9âz), P₂=ε₀(20ây+7.5âz). The free surface charge is 8ε₀ and the polarization surface charge is −6ε₀.

*Note: The y=5/y=3 inconsistency in Test 4 and the current arrow in the figure are present in the source; the y=3 used in the solution and the +z current direction stated in the text were kept.*
