## Quiz 1: Maxwell's equations

Type A asks for the differential and integral forms of the Ampère–Maxwell law and Gauss's law for electricity; Type B asks for those of Faraday's law and Gauss's law for magnetism. The basic expressions:

∇×H=J+∂D/∂t; ∇·D=ρ; ∇×E=−∂B/∂t; ∇·B=0.

## Quiz 1, A: square loop

Side a=2 m, rotation axis x, ω=4π×10³ rad/s; the initial position is the x-y plane. The +z field is 10⁻³ T, present during the interval 0<t<12.5×10⁻⁵ s.

Φ=4×10⁻³cos(4π×10³t), V=16πsin(4π×10³t) V. In the following interval the source sets the field and the voltage to zero.

## Quiz 1, B: triangular loop

An equilateral triangle of side a=√3 m rotates about the y axis at ω=10π×10³ rad/s. Initially it lies in the x-y plane; B=10⁻³ T is applied during 0<t<10⁻⁴ s.

S=3√3/4, Φ=(3√3/4)10⁻³cos(10π×10³t), V=7.5√3πsin(10π×10³t) V.

## Quiz 2, A: electric field given

ε=ε₀, E=36πcos(5π×10⁸t−5πx)â_y V/m. +x direction, f=250 MHz, v=10⁸ m/s, μ_r=9, η=360π Ω, λ=0.4 m.

Ẽ=36πâ_y e^(−j5πx), H̃=0.1â_z e^(−j5πx).

## Quiz 2, B: magnetic field given

ε=ε₀, H=â_z cos(10⁹πt−20πy)/(720π) A/m. +y direction, f=500 MHz, v=5×10⁷ m/s, μ_r=36, η=720π Ω, λ=0.1 m.

H̃=â_z e^(−j20πy)/(720π), Ẽ=−â_x e^(−j20πy), E=−â_x cos(10⁹πt−20πy) V/m.
