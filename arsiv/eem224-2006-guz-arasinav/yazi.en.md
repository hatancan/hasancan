## Plane wave in a magnetic dielectric
ε=2ε₀, μ=8μ₀; electric field in the +x direction, propagation in the +y direction. k=4k₀, η=2η₀. Ẽ=âxE₀exp(−j4k₀y), H̃=−âz[E₀/(2η₀)]exp(−j4k₀y). E=âxE₀cos(ωt−4k₀y). Instantaneous power flux ây[E₀²/(2η₀)]cos²(ωt−4k₀y).

## Good conductor
μ=μ₀, ε=36ε₀, σ=10 S/m; E=2exp(−αy)cos(2π×10⁸t−βy)âz. σ/(ωε)=50. Approximately α=β=20π; phase velocity 10⁷ m/s, λ=0.1 m, δ=1/(20π) m.

## Obliquely propagating wave
εr=9/4, μr=1; Ẽ=20×10⁻⁴η₀ âx exp[−j2π(3y−4z)]. Propagation unit vector 0.6ây−0.8âz; angle with the y-axis approximately 53°. β=10π, v=2×10⁸ m/s, λ=0.2 m, f=1 GHz; η=80π Ω. H̃=−10⁻³(2.4ây+1.8âz)exp[−j2π(3y−4z)].

## Reflection from a perfect conductor
In a medium with ε=4ε₀, μ=μ₀, Ẽᵢ=âxη₀exp[−j(4π/3)z], with a conducting boundary at z=0. f=100 MHz, λ=1.5 m, η=η₀/2. H̃ᵢ=2âyexp[−j(4π/3)z]. Reflected Ẽᵣ=−âxη₀exp[j(4π/3)z], H̃ᵣ=2âyexp[j(4π/3)z]. Total instantaneous E=2η₀âx sin(4πz/3)sinωt. The minimum of the magnetic field nearest the boundary is at a distance of λ/4=0.375 m.
