## A. Linking ideas (bağlantı ifadeleri)

**Yönerge:** Link the ideas in the sentences by adding an appropriate word or phrase from the list. Use each linker *ONLY ONCE*. Be careful, there are three extra ones.

Seçenekler: *let's get back to · as a rule · moreover · move on to · on the other hand · that covers · let's leave this · firstly · lastly · let's begin with*

> Hello and good morning. My name is Ahmet Güçlü. I am a senior engineering student at Başkent University. Today I would like to talk to you about my profession. My aim is to clarify what an engineer is and what kind of work we do. I have divided my talk into three sections. (1) **Firstly**, I will tell you what a typical engineer does. Then I will talk about how engineers work as individuals. (2) **Lastly**, we will discuss engineers working together. My speech will take approximately ten minutes. I will show you some charts and photos to help you better understand. If you have any questions please feel free to ask them at the end of my presentation. I realize many of you may not be acquainted with this topic. I assure you after I finish you will be quite familiar with it.
>
> (3) **Let's begin with** "What a Typical Engineer Does". Engineers are problem solvers, organizers, communicators, calculators and designers. Therefore they are skilled at clearly defining a problem and its relevant limitations (such as time, cost, etc.) and offering simple solutions. A senior engineer isn't usually responsible for most of the technical work. Technical work meaning calculations and designs. Instead he focuses on supervising a project or team of engineers.
>
> After this definition of "What an engineer does" I would like to (4) **move on to** "How Different Engineers Work Together" by giving an example such as Building a Tunnel.
>
> We call a civil engineer specializing in structural design a structural engineer. For example, he would calculate the size of the tunnel, how it could be constructed. In addition, he would figure out what this tunnel would be made of like reinforced concrete, etc. So as to provide clean air and remove as much of the vehicle emissions as possible, a mechanical engineer is brought in to design a ventilation system. The mechanical engineer would try to determine the amount of emissions that would need to be evacuated and the process of removing them. For instance, how many of a particular type of fan would be required and where they are to be installed. An electrical engineer would design the lighting as well as any control systems for hazard signs.
>
> (5) **Let's leave this** and continue with "Do Engineers Work as Individuals?"
>
> The answer to this question depends on the specific project, job and even field that engineers work in. But (6) **as a rule**, all engineers work as part of a small or large team. Chemical engineers conducting research and development may spend the majority of their time working independently in a lab doing experiments and writing technical reports. (7) **On the other hand** a civil engineer employed in the construction industry may spend the majority of his time on-site, working with foremen and tradesmen to strengthen and improve buildings or infrastructure in various ways.
>
> And (8) **that covers** all I have to say about engineers working as individuals.

## B. Writing: sunumu bitirmek

**Yönerge:** Now, write an ending/concluding part using the information given in the short presentation above.

Öğrencinin yazdığı kapanış:

> I have just mentioned what engineering is, which brings us to the end of my presentation. To sum up, today I talked about my profession. I clarified what an engineer is and what kind of work we do. Let me just run over the key points again. First I started with "What a typical engineer does". Then, I moved on to "How different engineers work together". And finally, I finished with "Do engineers work as individuals?". I hope that today you have gained insight into engineering. Thank you for your attention. If you have any questions I would be pleased to answer them. Any questions?

Kâğıdın altına not edilen kapanışın bölümleri:

| Summary | To sum up, today I talked about… |
