## Quiz 1: Maxwell's Equations

Complete the expressions ∇×H=J+∂D/∂t and ∇·D=ρ_v, and explain their integral forms and meanings.

∮H·dl=∫(J+∂D/∂t)·dS, ∮D·dS=∫ρ_v dv.

In the second question, a square loop with sides of 1 m rotates about the y axis at ω=100π rad/s. At t=0 it lies in the y-z plane. The field is in the +z direction at 4×10⁻³ T and becomes zero after 5×10⁻³ s. The source finds Φ=4×10⁻³sin(100πt) and, for the stated polarity, V=0.4πcos(100πt) V; in the following interval where the field is zero, V=0 is taken.

## Quiz 2: Plane Wave

In a medium with ε=9ε₀, E(y,t)=16πcos(4π×10⁸t−8πy)â_z V/m.

Propagation is in +y; f=200 MHz; v=5×10⁷ m/s; μ_r=4; η=80π Ω; λ=0.25 m.

Ẽ=16πâ_z e^(−j8πy), H̃=(1/5)â_x e^(−j8πy), H=0.2cos(4π×10⁸t−8πy)â_x A/m.

The second question is an explanation of the Maxwell and Gauss relations from the first quiz.

## Quiz 3: Normal Incidence and Power

For a wave incident normally from a dielectric with μ=μ₀ onto air, the E field is in the z direction and has unit amplitude. Since the reflected average power is 1/25 of the incident power, |Γ|=1/5. Choosing a positive Γ, the source finds ε_r=9/4, η₁=80π Ω, k₁=3k₀/2, τ=6/5.

Ẽ_i=â_z e^(−jk₁y), H̃_i=â_x e^(−jk₁y)/(80π).

Ẽ_r=â_z e^(jk₁y)/5, H̃_r=−â_x e^(jk₁y)/(400π).

Ẽ_t=(6/5)â_z e^(−jk₀y), H̃_t=â_x e^(−jk₀y)/(100π).

Directed powers for unit amplitude: +â_y/(160π), −â_y/(4000π), +3â_y/(500π) W/m². The directed sum of the incident and reflected powers equals the transmitted power.

## Quiz 3: Polarization

[4√2â_x+(4+4j)e^(jπ/4)â_z]e^(−jky) simplifies to 4√2(â_x+jâ_z)e^(−jky): circular polarization.

(2â_y+j5â_z)e^(jkx): the perpendicular components differ by 90° and their amplitudes are unequal; elliptical polarization. The source classifies these as right-handed/counterclockwise using the drawing along the propagation direction.
