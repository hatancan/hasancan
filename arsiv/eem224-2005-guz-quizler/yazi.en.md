## Quiz 1, Type A

In a medium with ε=ε₀, E(x,t)=360πcos(2×10⁹t−20x)â_y V/m. Solution: propagation in +x, v=10⁸ m/s, μ_r=9, η=360π Ω, λ=π/10 m. Ẽ=360πâ_y e^(−j20x), H=â_z cos(2×10⁹t−20x) A/m.

An equilateral triangular loop with sides of √3 m rotates about the y axis at 4π×10³ rad/s. B=10⁻³ T is applied during the interval 0<t<10⁻³ s. The area is S=3√3/4; with the source polarity taken into account, V=−3√3π sin(4π×10³t) V is obtained.

## Quiz 1, Type B

H(y,t)=−â_z cos(3×10⁹t−20y)/(24π) A/m and ε=ε₀. Solution: propagation in +y, v=1.5×10⁸ m/s, μ_r=4, η=240π Ω, λ=π/10 m. E=10â_x cos(3×10⁹t−20y) V/m.

A circular loop of radius 1 m rotates about the x axis at 6π rad/s. B increases linearly from 0 to 10⁻⁶ T over the interval 0–1.5 s. The flux is Φ=(2π/3)10⁻⁶t cos(6πt). For the direction of V of the source:

V=(2π/3)10⁻⁶cos(6πt)−4π²10⁻⁶t sin(6πt).

For R=1 kΩ, I=V/R. In the following interval, the solution takes the source V and I as zero.

## Quiz 2, Type A: Conducting Boundary

The solution sheets give an incidence angle of 30°, k=6π rad/m, f=900 MHz, and λ=1/3 m. The phasor of the incident electric field is:

Ẽ_i=(−3â_x+3√3â_y+3â_z)e^[−j(3π√3x+3πy)].

At the perfectly conducting boundary the tangential E vanishes. The reflected field is:

Ẽ_r=(−3â_x−3√3â_y−3â_z)e^[−j(−3π√3x+3πy)].

H̃ is found from H̃=(1/η₀)â_k×Ẽ.

In the normal incidence part, |Γ|²=1/16, Γ=−1/4, ε_r=25/9, τ=3/4, η₂=3η₀/5, and k₂=5k₀/3 are obtained. The average powers balance as 1/(2η₀), −1/(32η₀), 15/(32η₀).

## Quiz 2, Type B

In the oblique incidence solution, θ_i=60°, k=4π rad/m, f=600 MHz, λ=1/2 m. The incident and reflected E are:

Ẽ_i=(5â_x−5√3â_y+5â_z)e^[−j(2πy+2π√3z)],

Ẽ_r=(−5â_x+5√3â_y+5â_z)e^[−j(−2πy+2π√3z)].

In the normal incidence part, |Γ|²=1/25, Γ=−1/5, μ_r=9/4, and τ=4/5 are found. The solution emphasizes that the reflected power flux is written with a minus sign because its propagation direction is reversed.
