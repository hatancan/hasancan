EEM224 Electromagnetics I: April 2, 2013 Midterm Exam: Solution and Study Notes

EEM224 – Elektromanyetik I · 02.04.2013 · 17.10.2026

This archive entry, transcribed to text from photographed pages, contains the question texts along with the legible solution steps. The photographs are kept in a private archive; there is no downloadable file.

It was transcribed from a friend's course notes and answer pages; personal information has been removed. Despite the April 2014 file names, the exam title is dated April 2, 2013.

Vector operations

A=4âx−2ây−âz, B=âx+4ây−4âz, C=ây. A·B=4−8+4=0. A×C=âx+4âz.

Gradient and directional derivative

V=r sinφ+10. In cylindrical coordinates, ∇V=âr sinφ+âφ cosφ. Converted to Cartesian, ∇V=ây. The derivative in the +y direction is ∇V·ây=1.

Volume charge in a finite cylinder

For the charged cylinder between z=−2 and z=2, dq=ρv r′dr′dφ′dz′. In the working pages, the z coordinate of the observation point is 4 in one place and 6 in another; the radius is written as 5 and the angle as π/4. The difference vector from the source in the field integral is set up as âr(5−r′)+âz(6−z′).

Note: Because the cylindrical unit vectors at the source and observation points lie at different angles, this difference vector is in general not correct. The general squared distance should be 25+r′²−10r′cos(π/4−φ′)+(6−z′)². The missing angular dependence in the source solution is flagged here as a correction note; no uncertain result was produced.

Charged conducting spherical shell

Inner radius 3 m, outer radius 4 m; the initially uncharged shell is given a charge of 3 C, and there is no charge in the cavity. In the source, E=0 in the inner cavity and in the conductor; outside, E=3âR/(4πε₀R²) is intended to be written.

Note: In the handwriting, the boundary value 4² was used in the denominator of the outer-region result instead of R². 3/(64πε₀) is the field magnitude only at the surface R=4; it is not constant throughout the outer region.
