INTERNSHIP Summer Internship: Presentation on the Electrical-Electronics Engineering internship procedure and internship announcement (Başkent University)

STAJ – Yaz Stajı · 01.10.2013 · 14.11.2026

This post contains the text of the "Internship Procedure" presentation (16 slides, October 2013) prepared by Prof. Dr. Alper Uraz, Chair of the Internship Committee of the Electrical-Electronics Engineering Department at Başkent University, together with the internship announcement from the same period. The presentation walks students who will do an internship through the process: regulations, choosing a workplace, application, attendance, report, course registration, and evaluation. The original presentation can be downloaded as a PDF at the end of the post. For the department's internship guide, see the post EEM Staj Kılavuzu.

Regulations

  • Faculty of Engineering Internship Regulations
  • Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering Internship Guide
  • Each student must complete at least 2 internships during their studies, each lasting at least 20 working days.

Purpose of internships

The purpose is to observe on site how the theoretical knowledge learned in electronics engineering courses is put into practice through professional activities covering all fields of work, such as product development, production, and application, and to take part in the work to the extent the organizations allow.

Choosing a workplace

  • Medium-sized and large enterprises, factories, power plants, research institutions, and similar workplaces where electrical and electronic products are manufactured and applied are suitable for internships.
  • Workplaces that are not of a certain size; that are purely marketing-oriented; project-design-software firms; family businesses; and course-like activities are not suitable for internships.
  • Internship I and Internship II must be done at different workplaces.

Internship application steps

  • Verbal approval must be obtained for a prospective internship site.
  • Before starting the internship, the letter prepared in your name (Appendix-2 Special Letter) must be obtained from the department.
  • For the "Occupational Accident and Occupational Disease" insurance procedure, apply to the department chair's office one week in advance with a photocopy of your population registration certificate and a residence certificate.
  • The internship must be carried out in accordance with the Internship Regulations.

Attendance

  • Attending work during the internship is mandatory, except for health reasons.
  • The intern must demonstrate the responsibility and diligence of a prospective engineer.
  • The internship site is among the first places where you can apply for a job after graduation.
  • Keeping a daily internship logbook is not required.

End of the internship

At the end of the internship, each student is responsible for ensuring that the Internship Evaluation Form, which allows workplace officials to evaluate the hours worked and the work done, is completed and approved, and delivered to the department chair's office in a sealed envelope bearing the workplace stamp.

Internship report

Each student is responsible for preparing an internship report on their internship, in the format and with the content specified in the internship guide, and submitting it to the department chair's office. Internship reports must be submitted to the department chair's office no later than the 3rd week of the semester following the end of the internship.

Report content: Table of contents; (1) The workplace where the internship was done (in this section of at most 2 pages, the workplace is introduced in outline); (2) Work done during the internship (all work done is given in detail in chronological order); (3) Benefits gained from the internship; (if any) appendices.

The section on the workplace where the internship was done should include:

  • Name and address of the organization
  • A short history describing the development of the organization
  • The parent organization it is affiliated with, if any, and its existing facilities
  • Number of employees (workers, technicians, engineers, administrative staff, etc.)
  • Its status and (if not confidential) its capital structure
  • Fields of activity (R&D, design, production, assembly, maintenance and repair, etc.)

Registration for the internship course

  • In the semester following the end of the internship, each student must register for the course coded either EEM300 or EEM400, depending on their stage.
  • EEM300 is a prerequisite for EEM400.
  • Internship I can be done at the earliest after 4 semesters of coursework, and Internship II after 6 semesters.
  • Two internships cannot be done in the same summer period.
  • An internship can be done during the February term.

Internship evaluation

  • Each student's internship is evaluated by the Department Internship Committee on the basis of the Internship Report and the Internship Evaluation Form.
  • A grade of Y (satisfactory) is given in the registered course for a successful internship, and Z (unsatisfactory) for an unsuccessful one.
  • A student without an Internship Report and Evaluation Form receives a grade of Z.

Sample internship sites

  • Türk Telekomünikasyon A.Ş., TRT, State Airports Authority (DHMİ), PTT General Directorate, TEİAŞ General Directorate, TCDD, TÜBİTAK
  • Aselsan A.Ş., Havelsan A.Ş., Roketsan A.Ş., TAI-TUSAŞ Aerospace Industries Inc., Gate Elektronik A.Ş., Mikes Mikrodalga Elektronik Sis. A.Ş., Karel Elektronik San. ve Tic. A.Ş.
  • Arçelik A.Ş., Beko Elektronik A.Ş., Bosch Sanayi ve Ticaret A.Ş., Siemens A.Ş., Turkcell A.Ş., Türk Philips A.Ş., Vestel Elektronik San. ve Tic. A.Ş.

Internship announcement (to the attention of EEM students)

  1. Be sure to read the internship regulations.
  2. Obtain verbal approval from the department for a prospective internship site.
  3. Before starting the internship, obtain the letter prepared in your name by the department (Appendix-2 B Special Letter).
  4. For the "Occupational Accident and Occupational Disease" insurance procedure, apply to the department chair's office one week in advance with a photocopy of your population registration certificate and a residence certificate.
  5. Complete your internship in accordance with the regulations.
  6. Register for the EEM300/400 course.
  7. Submit the internship report to the department chair's office by 16:00 on the Friday of the 3rd week.
  8. Also make sure the internship evaluation form reaches the department.

The announcement was published over the signature of Prof. Dr. Alper Uraz, Chair of the Internship Committee.
