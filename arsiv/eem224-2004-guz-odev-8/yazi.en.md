## Input Impedance of a Lossy Line
Z₀ = 50 Ω, α = 1.5 dB/m, ZL = 100 Ω and λ = 2 m. The input impedance at a distance of 1 m from the load is required. α = 1.5 ln(10)/20 ≈ 0.1727 Np/m, β = π rad/m. The input relation is:

Zin = Z₀ [ZL + Z₀ tanh(γl)] / [Z₀ + ZL tanh(γl)].

For l = 1 m, tanh(γl) ≈ 0.171 and Zin ≈ 80.9 Ω.

## Voltage from the Load Reflection Coefficient
A source with a 20 V open-circuit voltage and 75 Ω source impedance is given, along with a line of 75 Ω characteristic impedance and ΓL = 0.45∠23°. From the relation ZL = Z₀(1+ΓL)/(1−ΓL), ZL is approximately 174.26∠23.8° Ω; in the solution, the magnitude of the load voltage is found to be 14.21 V.
